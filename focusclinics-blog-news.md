## What I filtered out and why

The cheap end of the blog/news pool is mostly unusable: Indian, Nigerian, Filipino and Kannada
publications whose categories do not name their country, automotive and weather sites with no
health angle, and `newsbreak.com` ($20, AS 60) which is a syndication aggregator rather than a
publisher. `evite.com` ($260, AS 58) is largely user-generated party pages — the same
platform-authority trap as `houzz.com.au` on another account.

Metrics are the marketplace sheet's own figures, not re-verified per domain; I contacted no
publisher.

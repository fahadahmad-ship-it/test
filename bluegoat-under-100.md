# bluegoatcyber.com — under $100

## The honest answer

**There is no cybersecurity or medtech inventory under $100 in this sheet.** 117 sub-$100
domains passed the quality gates; after removing UGC platforms, PR wires and link farms,
80 remained — and not one is a security or medical-device publication. For a US B2B firm
selling FDA-regulated medical device pen testing, this price band is the wrong shelf.

I checked all 80 against Ahrefs. Only `itechsoul.com` is already built, so the list is clean —
it is just not relevant.

## What the sub-$100 tier actually contains

| Flag | Count |
|---|---|

The clean 80 break down as: Indian and Pakistani tech blogs (`techmistri.com`,
`mobilemall.pk`, `hindiblogindia.com`), Russian IT sites (`ict-online.ru`, `spbit.ru`),
piracy-adjacent domains (`isaimini.ltd`, `sociallykeeda.com`), and consumer niches with no
bearing on the client — fonts, Minecraft modpacks, EVs, MMA, wine.

## Two traps I checked and rejected

**`thecyber.tech.blog` ($45, AS 41, 51,717 traffic)** looked like the find of the set — a
cybersecurity site with real authority for $45. `tech.blog` is an Automattic platform that
sells subdomains. This is a hosted blog, and the AS 41 belongs to the platform, not the blog.
Same trap as `houzz.com.au` and `gardenweb.com` in the other account.

**`addmagazine.co.uk` ($30)** is the same guest-post vendor inventory I flagged on the other
client — sold across multiple link networks at a near-identical price.

## What I would actually do

If $100 is a hard per-link cap, **`dentaltown.com` at $70 is the only purchase I would defend** —
a real US medical-professional audience, which is at least the right room even if the topic is
dental rather than device security.

If $100 is the total budget, my recommendation is to **not spend it here and save toward one
relevant placement instead**. From the main list:

- `medindia.net` **$250** — AS 61, 613k traffic, medical
- `microbenotes.com` **$350** — AS 49, 796k traffic, science/medicine
- `cybernews.com` **$582** — AS 65, 3.4M traffic, cybersecurity

One `medindia.net` link is worth more to this client than every sub-$100 site on the sheet
combined. Given the profile already carries 2,581 referring domains against ~300 monthly
organic visits, adding more low-relevance links is the opposite of what this site needs.

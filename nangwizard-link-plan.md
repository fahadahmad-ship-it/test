## 3. Problems to fix first (critical)

### 3a. Toxic / spam links — audit & disavow
Ahrefs flags several referring domains as spam, plus two clear manipulation patterns:

- **Negative-SEO / vendor-spam anchor:** ~150 referring domains carry the identical spun anchor *"I remember when nangwizard.net was hardly visible… after discovering SEOExpress.org…"* (all nofollow, DR≤41). This is a third-party SEO vendor spamming nangwizard.net as a **testimonial prop for their own site.** Not built by the client, but it bloats the profile with junk.
- **PBN / "buy backlinks" footprint anchors:** *"High Quality Dofollow Backlinks DA 50 PA 40 Premium PBN Network…"* (28 ref domains) and a *"we deliver guest posts and backlinks…"* anchor (16 ref domains, DR ~0.8).
- **Spam-flagged domains to review for disavow:** `rankyour.website`, `factmags.com`, `buybacklinks.agency`, `articlement.com`, `kingranks.com` (4 links), `rank-top.click`, `4guysfromrolla.com`.

**Action:** Export full backlinks, filter `is_spam = true` + the PBN/testimonial anchor clusters, and submit a **disavow file** (domain-level) in Google Search Console. Re-review quarterly. Do this *before* pouring budget into new links — you're currently paying an authority "tax" on spam.

### 3b. Anchor over-optimisation
Exact-match commercial anchors are already over-represented. **Cap new exact-match anchors and shift the mix** (see §6).

### 3c. Link churn
2,487 all-time vs 820 live ref domains = heavy loss, typical of guest-post/PR links that get pruned. Favour **durable link types** (business citations, profiles, genuine editorial) over here-today-gone-tomorrow syndication.

## 5. Link opportunity list (prioritised)

Prioritised for **relevance, durability, safety, and replicability of the leader's edge.** Tiers = do in order.

### Tier 1 — Foundational citations & business profiles (do now; cheap, durable, safe)
Brand/naked anchors only. These close the biggest measurable gap vs nangsdelivery.com.

- **AU local citations (highest relevance):** Google Business Profile, Bing Places, True Local, Yellow Pages AU, Hotfrog AU, StartLocal, Yelp AU, Aussie Web, Local Search, dLook, Word of Mouth, Cylex AU, PureLocal, AussieWeb.
- **Global business directories the leader uses:** provenexpert.com, birdeye.com, theorg.com, bunity.com, diib.com, bizratings.com, dealerbaba.com, zipleaf.com, semfirms.com, apsense.com.
- **Profile / Web 2.0 (brand hubs):** beacons.ai, bigcartel.com, about.me (already have — optimise it), disqus.com, slides.com, instapaper.com, telegra.ph, gravatar, behance, flickr.
- **Review platforms:** ProductReview.com.au (critical for AU e-commerce trust), Trustpilot, Google reviews.

### Tier 2 — Niche & contextual relevance (the real ranking lever)
The current profile is broad-lifestyle but thin on *food-tech / hospitality / bartending / dessert* context — the topically-relevant signal for cream chargers.

- **Culinary / dessert / food-science angle:** guest articles or product mentions on food blogs, dessert/baking sites, molecular-gastronomy and cocktail/mixology blogs (whipped cream, espuma, foams, infusions). You already have `juliescafebakery.com`, `beerconnoisseur.com`, `porchdrinking.com`, edible* titles — get more of this *type*.
- **Hospitality & events:** catering, party-hire, event-planning, cocktail-bar resources (AU-focused where possible).
- **AU lifestyle / city guides:** "best late-night delivery in Melbourne", nightlife and student-city round-ups — strong local + topical relevance.
- **Reclaim competitor guest-post placements** you don't yet have but the leader does and that are genuinely editorial: `nerdbot.com`, `urbansplatter.com` (already have), `ocnjdaily.com`, `iamrestaurant.com` (restaurant-relevant — good target).

### Tier 3 — Digital PR / linkable assets (builds DR + natural anchors at scale)
Turn existing guides into link magnets and pitch them (data > product pages earn editorial links and dilute commercial anchors):

- You already rank/own guides: *"the history of nangs"*, *"are nangs illegal in Australia"*, *"what are nangs"*. **Expand these into genuinely citable resources** and pitch to journalists:
  - **Legal/safety explainer** — "Nangs & the law in Australia, state by state" (updated). News + .edu/.gov-adjacent harm-reduction sites link to authoritative legal explainers (note: harm-reduction orgs like `adf.org.au`, `cahma.org.au` already rank in this space and attract links).
  - **Culinary use guide** — professional whipped-cream / espuma techniques, charger types (MOSA, 580g tanks), safety. Pitch to food media.
  - **Data / trend piece** — e.g. delivery-demand trends by city; pitch to AU lifestyle/news desks.
- **HARO / Qwoted / SourceBottle (AU)** — respond as a hospitality-supply expert for editorial mentions.
- **Targeted press release** (branded anchors) via a reputable wire for a genuine news hook (new city launch, wholesale line) — mirrors the leader's PR pillar. Use sparingly; one strong hook beats mass syndication.

### Tier 4 — Supplier / partner / B2B (wholesale side)
The site has a `/wholesale/` page — leverage B2B relationships:
- Cream-charger and whipper **brand/stockist pages** (e.g. MOSA distributor listings), hospitality-supply marketplaces, café/bar supplier directories, "where to buy" pages.
- Local partnerships: catering companies, party-hire, event venues → resource-page and partner links.

### Avoid / do NOT do
- ❌ Spam bookmark & auto-"social" networks (the `bookmark*.com` / `*social*.com` clusters nangsboy and nangsdelivery both use). High footprint, ~zero durable value, adds risk.
- ❌ Any "buy backlinks / PBN / DA50 PA40" vendor — this footprint is already partly in your profile and is a liability.
- ❌ Fresh exact-match commercial anchors at scale (see §6).
- ❌ Foreign-language / off-topic guest-post farms with no Australian or culinary relevance.

## 6. Anchor text plan for NEW links

Shift the incoming mix to look natural and dilute existing over-optimisation:

| Anchor type | Target share of new links | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Branded | ~40% | Nang Wizard, nangwizard, Nang Wizard delivery |
| Naked URL | ~25% | nangwizard.net, https://www.nangwizard.net |
| Generic / natural | ~15% | "this delivery service", "click here", "learn more", "order here" |
| Partial / topical | ~15% | "fast nang delivery in Melbourne", "cream charger delivery service" |
| Exact-match commercial | **≤5%** | "nangs delivery", "cream chargers" — use rarely, only on high-trust editorial |

**Rule:** Tier 1 & Tier 4 links = branded/naked only. Reserve the few exact-match anchors for Tier 2/3 high-DR editorial placements.

## 7. 90-day roadmap

**Month 1 — Clean up + foundation**
- Full backlink audit; build & submit disavow (spam-flagged domains + PBN/testimonial anchor clusters).
- Claim/optimise Tier 1 AU citations + core global profiles (NAP-consistent). Target 25–40 durable citation/profile links.
- Set up ProductReview.com.au, Trustpilot, GBP review flow.

**Month 2 — Relevance**
- 4–6 topically-relevant placements (culinary/hospitality/AU-city) with branded/partial anchors.
- Reclaim/pursue genuinely editorial competitor domains (iamrestaurant.com, nerdbot.com, ocnjdaily.com, restaurant/food titles).
- Begin HARO/SourceBottle responses (aim 2–3 mentions).

**Month 3 — PR / assets**
- Publish 1–2 upgraded linkable assets (legal explainer + culinary guide).
- One well-hooked press release (branded anchors).
- Outreach to AU lifestyle/news for the asset(s); target 5–10 editorial links.

**Ongoing KPIs:** DR trend (target 25 → 30+ in ~2 quarters), live ref domains net-positive (fix churn), % branded/naked anchors up, spam links down, movement on "nangs" (8→top 5) and "cream chargers" (8→top 5), city pages (Sydney/Brisbane pos 6–9 → top 5).

---

## 8. Strategic summary

1. **You don't need more links — you need cleaner, more relevant, more durable ones.** Volume is already there; trust and topical relevance are the gaps.
2. **Close the leader's real edge:** business profiles/citations + selective PR, *not* bookmark spam.
3. **Fix the anchor mix** — you're one algorithm update away from an over-optimisation problem on "nangs delivery" / "cream chargers".
4. **Disavow the spam** the domain has accumulated (incl. the SEOExpress testimonial-spam cluster) before spending on acquisition.
5. **Build topical authority** via culinary/hospitality/legal-explainer content — that's what lifts "nangs" and "cream chargers" (the big-volume heads) rather than just the already-won branded/local terms.

*Note on compliance:* Nang Wizard positions and sells these as culinary cream chargers / N₂O for food use. Keep outreach and on-page messaging in the culinary/hospitality framing, and keep the Australia-by-state legal explainer accurate and current — it doubles as your best linkable asset and your compliance shield.

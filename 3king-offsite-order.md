## WHY EACH WAS SELECTED

### 1 — `https://3king.cc/` · anchor `3King`
A **DR 0** domain (`3king-game.com`) holds **#1** on your brand name;
`3king-game-mobile.com` (DR 3) is #2; you are **#3 at DR 19** with 2,683 referring
domains, capturing roughly **7%** of your own brand SERP. Losing to sites with no
link equity means the gap is entity association, not authority — the fastest and
cheapest thing on this list to shift. Brand traffic also converts at full rate.

**Anchor reasoning:** the brand **word**, never the string `3king.cc`. That string
is already your largest spam fingerprint — 40 referring domains, Ahrefs
`is_spam=true`, only **2 dofollow**. The clone wins this SERP on 319 referring
domains carrying the clean anchor `3KING` (1,892 dofollow) against your 2. This
link starts closing that specific gap.

### 2 — `https://3king.cc/` · anchor `tải game 3King`
Download is the highest-LTV conversion in this vertical. The homepage **already
ranks #3 for `tải game 3king`** and #2 for `tai 3king` / `tải 3king` — so this is
reinforcing a position you hold, not creating one from nothing. That makes it the
highest-probability ranking gain in the set.

**Anchor reasoning:** brand-led with download intent. The clone holds 279
referring domains on this exact string, so place **one** instance only — do not
repeat at volume.

**Why two links to `/`:** the homepage is where both brand and download intent
live, and it is the URL the brand SERP is actually being lost on. Different
anchors, so no footprint issue.

### 3 — `https://3king.cc/ban-ca` · anchor `game bắn cá đổi thưởng 3King`
**The single best non-brand keyword in the account.** It is the only commercial
term I tested whose Semrush SERP contains legitimate ranking websites —
`gamevui.vn` (#6), `gameshub.com/vn/nha-cai-ban-ca-uy-tin/` (#7), `ica.net.vn`
(#10). It carries the **highest CPC in the dataset at $2.16**, which is the market
pricing real deposit intent, and KD 49 is reachable.

Every alternative was rejected on SERP evidence: `nổ hũ đổi thưởng`,
`game nổ hũ đổi thưởng` and `game đổi thưởng` all return hacked foreign domains
serving `/vi-vn/` paths, and `bắn cá đổi thưởng` (KD 80) is an app-store SERP.

**Anchor reasoning:** brand-qualified rather than bare exact-match. With only ~16
countable links in the profile, a bare money anchor is over-optimisation shape for
no gain.

**Known limitation:** the page is titled "miễn phí" (free) while the keyword means
real-money prize exchange. Without an onsite edit this link will build topical
relevance to the bắn cá cluster but is unlikely to rank the page for the exact
term. Accepted as a deliberate trade-off.

### 4 — `https://3king.cc/no-hu` · anchor `nổ hũ đổi thưởng 3King`
**The best page-to-keyword match on the site.** The title is "Chơi Nổ hũ online –
Đổi thưởng uy tín số 1 Việt Nam" — it already contains both target terms, so no
onsite work is required. `nổ hũ` is also the largest category in the vertical at
165,000/mo.

**Honest expectation:** the `nổ hũ đổi thưởng` head SERP is parasite-occupied
(`armavirdc.org`, `1xbet888.net/vi-vn/`, `nohu.sbs/vi-vn/`, `www-bet365.co.com`),
so this link is **not expected to rank the page for the head term**. It is bought
to build topical authority across the nổ hũ cluster and to balance the link
distribution across your two money categories rather than concentrating
everything on bắn cá. Judge it on cluster movement, not on this one keyword.

### 5 — `https://3king.cc/su-kien-khuyen-mai` · anchor `khuyến mãi 3King`
Highest-converting intent on the site — promo and registration. The page exists
at UR 4.8, is correctly titled, and `3king-game-mobile.com/khuyen-mai-3/` ranks
for your own promo terms while this page does not.

**Anchor reasoning:** `khuyến mãi 3King` rather than `3king tặng 30k`, because the
page does not mention "30k" anywhere. Anchoring to a term the page does not
contain creates a mismatch that dilutes the link. This anchor matches the page
title exactly and still carries the promo intent.

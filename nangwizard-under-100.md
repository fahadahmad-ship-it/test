## Three traps in this band

**`mymedical.health.blog` ($37)** is categorised "Food & Drink, Recipes" and looks like a
bargain. `health.blog` is an Automattic platform that sells subdomains — this is a hosted blog,
and the authority belongs to the platform. Identical to the `thecyber.tech.blog` trap I flagged
on another account.

**`ko-fi.com` ($36, AS 53, TF 56, 826,013 visits)** and **`indiehackers.com` ($30, AS 56)** are
the standout metrics in the whole band. Both are user-generated platforms — creator profiles and
community posts. The score is the platform's, not your placement's.

**`wellhealthorganick.com` ($20)** carries a 10 RD/1k ratio, which normally reads clean. The
name tracks a known content-farm brand family; at $20 with AS 26 there is nothing here worth the
risk to a profile that already carries heavy PBN pollution.

## My actual recommendation

Spend the $100 band sparingly. This site already has **1,137 live referring domains against
2,850 all-time**, and a large share of the live profile is PBN junk — hundreds of
`*-link-baron-*.store`, `seoexpress.*` and numeric `.xyz` domains. Adding more low-authority
links to a profile with that footprint is the wrong direction.

If the budget genuinely is sub-$100 per link, take `thechocolatelife.com` and
`cocktailsandshots.com` for topical relevance, and put the rest toward one proper Australian
placement — `horsedeals.com.au` $395 or `farmonlineweather.com.au` $545 — which is what this
profile actually lacks.

Metrics are the marketplace sheet's own figures, not re-verified per domain; I contacted no
publisher.

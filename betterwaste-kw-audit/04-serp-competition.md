# SERP competition check — who actually ranks, and what's realistically winnable

**Date:** 1 October 2026 · **Source:** Semrush UK, live top-10 organic per keyword
**Scope:** 24 decision-critical keywords across all six clusters

---

## Why this changes the plan

KD scores said Cluster A (core commercial terms) was moderate at KD 25–34. The actual
SERPs say otherwise. **The single biggest determinant of difficulty in this vertical
is not KD — it's how many council and gov.uk results occupy the top 10.** Those slots
are effectively unwinnable for a commercial operator, so a SERP with six of them is a
SERP with four real positions, two of which Biffa and Veolia already hold.

I've scored each keyword by what's actually in the top 10:

- **Blocked slots** — gov.uk, council/local authority, recyclenow, WRAP
- **Incumbent slots** — Biffa, Veolia, Suez, PHS, Initial, Stericycle, Shred-it, Restore
- **Open slots** — small and mid-size specialists (the ones Better Waste can displace)

A SERP with 5+ open slots and a visible small-operator presence is genuinely winnable
at DR 26. A SERP with 0–2 open slots is not, whatever the KD says.

## What the SERPs actually show

### 🔴 Cluster A is harder than KD suggests — I was wrong to rank it P1

`commercial waste collection` (3,600 SV, KD 25) returns:

> 1. biffa.co.uk · 2. veolia.co.uk · 3. **gov.uk** · 4. **ipswich.gov.uk** ·
> 5. **hillingdon.gov.uk** · 6. businesswaste.co.uk · 7. **belfastcity.gov.uk** ·
> 8. suez.co.uk · 9. **dudley.gov.uk** · 10. **preston.gov.uk**

Six of ten are councils or gov.uk. Biffa, Veolia and Suez hold three more. That leaves
**one** slot, held by businesswaste.co.uk at DR 72. `commercial waste management`,
`commercial waste disposal`, `trade waste collection` and `small business waste collection`
all follow the same shape.

**This is a real reversal of my earlier recommendation.** I had Cluster A as P1 on the
basis of volume and CPC. The SERP data says the Better Waste doc's instinct to defer
these was right, and I'd now sequence them later. The one exception is
`commercial rubbish removal` (320 SV, KD 18) — junkwize.com, chuckit.co.uk and
ukcommercialgroup.co.uk all rank, so that one is live.

### ⛔ Clinical and hazardous waste are household-intent SERPs

I flagged `bulky waste collection` as council-dominated. The same test applies to two
keywords I'd put at P1/P2 and didn't check:

`clinical waste collection` → cornwall.gov.uk, **gov.uk/request-clinical-waste-collection**,
rbwm.gov.uk, bexley.gov.uk, towerhamlets.gov.uk, leicester.gov.uk — six council/gov
results, and the gov.uk URL is literally a household collection request form.

`hazardous waste collection` → cityoflondon.gov.uk, gov.uk, islington.gov.uk,
recyclenow.com, redbridge.gov.uk — same picture.

**Both should drop out of the priority list.** The commercial demand for these services
is real, but it isn't expressed through these head terms. If Better Waste wants the
clinical stream, the entry point is `clinical waste disposal companies` or sector terms
like `dental waste disposal`, not the head term.

### 🟢 The city pages are more winnable than anything else

`commercial waste collection leeds`:

> 1. **leeds.gov.uk** · 2. **sensawaste.com** · 3. businesswaste.co.uk ·
> 4. liliwaste.co.uk · 5. thewasteteam.co.uk · 7. lsswaste.co.uk · 8. divert.co.uk

`commercial waste collection sheffield`:

> 1. **sheffield.gov.uk** · 2. **sensawaste.com** · 3. biffa.co.uk ·
> 4. sommerswastesolutions.com · 5. direct365.co.uk · 6. businesswaste.co.uk ·
> 9. kcmwaste.com

**sensawaste.com ranks #2 on both.** Semrush has it at 1,285 organic keywords and
1,319 traffic — a smaller site than Better Waste on most measures. It is beating
Biffa and Veolia on these terms with nothing but a dedicated city page. liliwaste,
thewasteteam, lsswaste, sommerswaste and kcmwaste are all small regional operators
holding top-10 slots.

This is the clearest proof in the whole dataset that **a dedicated, properly-built
city page wins at Better Waste's authority level.** The city terms have lower volume
(110–260) but $28–$35 CPC and a demonstrably open SERP.

### 🟢 Sector pages are soft

`butchers waste collection` — jgpears.com, affordablewastemanagement.co.uk,
wastemanaged.co.uk, wheeldonbrothers.co.uk, martlands.co.uk, easywaste.biz. No
councils bar one, no national brands at all. **Nine open slots.**

`care home waste management` — Stericycle and PHS hold two, but busybins.co.uk and
bwsl.co.uk rank at 6 and 9. Winnable.

`office waste management` — batesoffice.co.uk, tj-waste.co.uk, envirocraft.co.uk and
fswaste.co.uk all rank. The #1 is a businesswaste.co.uk *waste-facts* page, which is a
weak incumbent for a commercial term.

`school waste management` — the top 10 includes two PDFs, a World Economic Forum page
and a headteacher trade magazine. Very soft, but note the intent is Informational:
treat it as a lead-gen/authority page, not a conversion page.

### 🟡 Confidential and sanitary waste — open, but check the service fit first

Neither SERP has a single council result, which is unusual and good.

`confidential waste disposal` → homeshreduk.co.uk, secure-shred.co.uk,
printwaste.co.uk, shredstation.co.uk, emergerecycling.co.uk — mid-size shredding
specialists, not waste giants. Suez and Restore are the only heavyweights.

`sanitary waste disposal` → PHS and Initial lead, but trusthygiene.co.uk,
wgswaste.co.uk, hygienecowashrooms.co.uk, mustangwashrooms.com, flushhygiene.co.uk
and anwaywashrooms.co.uk all hold slots.

> ⚠️ **Commercial caveat, not an SEO one:** both are specialist service lines.
> Sanitary/washroom in particular is a distinct operation (bin servicing on a
> scheduled cycle, consumables, hygiene compliance). Worth confirming Better Waste
> actually delivers these before building pages for them — the SERP is open, but
> ranking for a service you can't fulfil is worse than not ranking.

### ⚠️ Bin-spec terms are retail SERPs

`1100 litre bin` returns wheeliebinwarehouse.com, wheeliebinsolutions.co.uk,
bigdug.co.uk, bin-shop.co.uk, officebins.co.uk — people buying a bin, not hiring a
collection service. Easy to rank, low commercial value.

**But `commercial bin sizes` is different and genuinely good:** businesswaste.co.uk is
#1, and **gwrwastemanagement.co.uk (DR 14)** is #3 with ashwasteservices.co.uk (DR 29)
at #5. A DR 14 site ranking top-3 is the strongest "this is winnable" signal in the
dataset. Build the bin-sizes guide; skip the individual product-spec pages.

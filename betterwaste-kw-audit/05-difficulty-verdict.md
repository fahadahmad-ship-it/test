## The test I'm applying

My first two passes scored difficulty on Semrush KD, then on how many council/gov
results held the SERP. Both were incomplete. KD is a model output, and counting
council results tells you which slots are blocked but not whether the *remaining*
slots are reachable.

The decisive question for a DR 26 site is simpler and testable:

> **Is there at least one site with DR ≤ 30 currently ranking in the top 10?**
> And if so, how many?

If sites weaker than Better Waste already rank, the SERP is reachable on content and
relevance alone. If every result is DR 45+, no amount of on-page work closes the gap
and the keyword needs link acquisition first.

| Grade | Rule | What it means for planning |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Easy** | 3+ results at DR ≤ 30 | Build the page; expect to rank on content quality |
| 🟡 **Medium** | 1–2 results at DR ≤ 30 | Build it, but it needs a genuinely strong page and some links |
| 🔴 **Hard** | 0 results at DR ≤ 30, or 4+ council/gov slots | Defer until DR improves |
| ⛔ **Avoid** | SERP intent ≠ commercial waste service | Don't target at all |

**This overturns several of my own earlier calls**, in both directions. Corrections
are marked ⚠️ below.

  `commercial waste collection liverpool` / `nottingham`, `commercial glass collection`,
  `hotel waste management`). Those are graded on the Semrush top-10 domain list alone
  and are marked as such in the CSV — treat them as slightly less certain.

  marks these `inherited` rather than claiming direct evidence.
- I could not crawl betterwaste.co.uk (network policy blocks the host), so page
  existence is inferred from indexed-URL data. The Better Waste doc states eleven
  sector pages already exist — if correct, several `CREATE` rows should read
  `OPTIMISE`, which makes them cheaper than scored here.

# v2 — Re-graded on Semrush Authority Score

Everything above was graded on Ahrefs DR. Re-run on Semrush AS, which is the metric
you actually work in. Two things changed materially.

## Better Waste is AS 20, not DR 26

The benchmark moved. The test is now:

> **How many sites at Authority Score ≤ 25 — at or below Better Waste — currently rank
> in the top 10?**

| Grade | Rule |
|---|---|
| 🟢 EASY | 3+ ranking sites at AS ≤ 25, and fewer than 4 council/gov slots |
| 🟡 MEDIUM | 1–2 at AS ≤ 25, **or** lowest AS is 26–30 with fewer than 4 council/gov slots |
| 🔴 HARD | Nothing at AS ≤ 25, or 4+ council/gov slots |
| ⛔ AVOID | SERP intent is not commercial waste service |
| ⚪ UNVERIFIED | Insufficient AS data to grade honestly |

## AS vs DR — where the two metrics disagreed

Mostly they agreed. Four grades moved:

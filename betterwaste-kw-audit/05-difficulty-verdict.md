---

## The test

Semrush KD is a model output, and counting council results tells you which slots are
blocked but not whether the remaining ones are reachable. The decisive question for an
AS 20 site is simpler and directly testable:

> **How many sites at Authority Score ≤ 25 — at or below Better Waste — currently rank
> in the top 10?**

If sites weaker than Better Waste already rank, the SERP is reachable on content and
relevance alone. If everything in the top 10 is AS 39+, no amount of on-page work
closes the gap and the keyword needs links first.

| Grade | Rule | What it means for planning |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **EASY** | 3+ ranking sites at AS ≤ 25, and fewer than 4 council/gov slots | Build it; expect to rank on content quality |
| 🟡 **MEDIUM** | 1–2 at AS ≤ 25, **or** lowest AS is 26–30 with fewer than 4 council/gov slots | Build it, but it needs a strong page and some links |
| 🔴 **HARD** | Nothing at AS ≤ 25, or 4+ council/gov slots | Defer until authority improves |
| ⛔ **AVOID** | SERP intent is not a commercial waste service | Don't target at all |

**Every one of the 104 keywords is now graded** — 36 from a direct SERP + Authority
Score check, 68 inherited from a directly-checked keyword on the same SERP (the
evidence cell names which). Authority Scores were pulled for 110 ranking domains.

# Ultimate Rides — Backlink Target Audit (Reach Gorilla sheet)

**Target site:** ultimaterides.com — a US lifted-truck dealership (lifted/custom Ford, Chevy, GMC trucks, SUVs, Jeep Wranglers; used trucks; nationwide delivery).
**Ideal link niche:** trucks / pickups / diesel / off-road / Ford-Chevy-GM / car buying & reviews, **US-focused**.
**Scope:** 73 "Level 4 Link (standard niche)" domains scheduled Mar '25 → Nov '26.
**Data:** Domain Rating (DR) pulled **live from Ahrefs**; AS (Semrush Authority Score), TF/CF (Majestic Trust/Citation Flow), Traffic, RD, Price taken from the uploaded vendor sheet.

> How to read the flags: **DR≫AS** = Ahrefs DR sits 30+ points above Semrush Authority Score. That gap is the classic fingerprint of a site whose authority was *built through link schemes / guest-post networks* rather than earned editorially — real-world value is usually closer to the lower AS. Majestic TF≈CF across the list (no severe toxicity signal), so the main risks here are **relevance**, **inflated DR**, and **UGC/directory/syndication** sources.

---

## Bottom line (critical)

- **Relevance is the biggest problem.** Only ~**22 of 73 (30%)** sites are genuinely automotive (truck/car). The other **~70%** are generic local news, student papers, or lifestyle/business/music/parenting blogs. For a campaign labelled *"standard niche,"* this reads much more like a **generic DR-chasing profile** than a truck/automotive-relevant one.
- **5 bullseye targets** (lock these in): **fordauthority.com, gmauthority.com, pickuptrucktalk.com, dieselworldmag.com, motortopia.com** — exact match to lifted trucks / Ford / GM / diesel / enthusiast.
- **~9 should be dropped or challenged** as directory/UGC/pay-to-publish or metrics that don't reconcile (see AVOID), led by **merchantcircle.com** (directory profile link, DR is vanity), **livepositively.com / elevatedmagazines.com** (open user-generated platforms), and **autoclassmagazine.com** (real DR **14** — vendor's AS 30 / 22k traffic don't add up).
- **~10 "high-DR" news sites carry a DR≫AS gap** — safe-ish but understand you're buying an inflated number, not editorial relevance.
- **~13 sites are off-geo** (UK, IT, DE, Georgia, Nigeria, Caribbean, Scandinavia, AU) — weak local signal for a US nationwide dealer.
- **Price sanity:** several are over-priced for what they are — smmirror.com ($265, local), easyreadernews.com ($240, local), unfinishedman.com ($267, lifestyle), kentuckynewera.com ($242) — while the best-value relevant buys are cleanfleetreport.com ($40), speedwaymedia.com ($50), bullz-eye.com ($93), gmauthority.com ($285 but worth it).

## Must-keep (PRIORITY — exact niche)
- **fordauthority.com** (DR71) — Ford trucks, your exact inventory. Best single target.
- **gmauthority.com** (DR74) — GM/Chevy trucks, exact brand match.
- **pickuptrucktalk.com** (DR48) — pickups, bullseye.
- **dieselworldmag.com** (DR46) — diesel/off-road trucks, bullseye.
- **motortopia.com** (DR40) — custom/enthusiast vehicles (DR modest, but relevance justifies it).

## Drop or challenge (AVOID)
- **merchantcircle.com** — business *directory*; the link is a profile listing (typically nofollow/UGC). DR 85 is meaningless here. $30 reflects it.
- **livepositively.com**, **elevatedmagazines.com** — open "publish your own article" platforms; DR inflated (DR≫AS +38 / +32), $30 each.
- **autoclassmagazine.com** — real DR **14**; vendor AS 30 & 22k traffic don't reconcile → authority effectively nil.
- **thebossmagazine.com** (DR≫AS +40, 4.4k traffic), **we-heart.com** (+35), **miamilivingmagazine.com** (+35, 4.2k), **whatstrending.com** (+32), **thehometrotters.com** (+33, 3.2k) — link-built authority + thin traffic + off-topic.
- **editorialge.com** (CAUTION) — general site based in the country of Georgia; DR 79 but no TF/traffic recorded — typical high-DR guest-post farm; verify before use.

## Use sparingly / understand what you're buying
- **Motorsport (grandprix247, speedwaydigest, speedwaymedia, autoracing1, f1chronicle):** automotive-adjacent but racing fans are not lifted-truck buyers — low commercial relevance. Fine for 1–2 diversity links, not a theme.
- **Local news & student papers (signalscv, muddyrivernews, fingerlakes1, dailyiowan, dailyemerald, thecoastnews, hometownstation, edhat, smmirror, nativenewsonline, harlemworldmagazine, cuindependent, caribbeannationalweekly, kentuckynewera, inkl):** legitimate outlets, safe for DR diversity, but **no topical relevance** to trucks. Don't let these dominate the profile. Many also show the DR≫AS gap.
- **Off-geo (mobypark DE, scandasia, tekedia NG, caranalytics UK, breakeryard UK, bestsellingcarsblog, focus2move IT, racingsportscars EU, drivesmartbc CA):** weaker local signal for a US dealer.
- **bignewsnetwork.com** — press-release *syndication wire*; DR 77 overstates real editorial value.

## Recommendations
1. **Rebalance toward the niche.** Right now ~70% of placements have no truck/automotive relevance. For a truck dealer, aim for a majority of links on automotive/truck/off-road sites; cap generic news + lifestyle at a minority used purely for DR/anchor diversity.
2. **Lock the 5 PRIORITY + 10 STRONG automotive sites first** — these do the real topical work.
3. **Cut the 9 AVOID sites** (directory/UGC/pay-to-publish + autoclassmagazine). Reallocate that budget to more truck/off-road/4x4/overlanding/car-review placements.
4. **Push back on price** for low-relevance, high-cost buys (smmirror $265, easyreadernews $240, unfinishedman $267, kentuckynewera $242).
5. **Treat DR with suspicion where DR≫AS by 30+.** Prefer sites where DR and AS agree *and* real organic traffic is healthy (e.g. theautochannel, focus2move, gmauthority, fordauthority).
6. **Geo:** prefer US sites; use UK/EU/other only when the automotive relevance is strong (e.g. racingsportscars, caranalytics).

*DR is live Ahrefs data; AS/TF/CF/Traffic/RD/Price are the vendor's own figures from the uploaded sheet and were not independently re-verified (Ahrefs paid units were exhausted, so organic-traffic numbers could not be cross-checked). Relevance/verdict judgments are editorial.*

## Buying notes

**`radiologykey.com`, `musculoskeletalkey.com` and `plasticsurgerykey.com` are one network** —
the same `*key.com` medical-content footprint. Unbuilt for this client, but buy **at most one**.

**`clinichunter.com`** is a medical-tourism platform that routes patients to overseas clinics,
eye surgery included — you would be paying to appear on a site competing for your patients.

**`walkindermatology.com`** is a working dermatology clinic. Clinic-to-clinic links are an
unusual editorial pattern; check what the placement actually is.

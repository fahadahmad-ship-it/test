### Rules applied to all four pages
- **No duplication.** Each page covers a distinct product family in its own words;
  near-duplicate local pages get filtered by Google. Shared facts (service area,
  brand) are phrased differently on each.
- **Local signals:** Peachtree City + Fayette County + the real service-area towns
  the site already lists (Newnan, Fayetteville, Tyrone, Senoia, Sharpsburg,
  Chattahoochee Hills). NAP and "free in-home consultation" CTA on every page.
- **Schema:** each page gets `Service` + `BreadcrumbList`; the hub page adds
  `LocalBusiness`; FAQ blocks get `FAQPage`.
- **Internal linking:** the hub page (`/window-treatments-peachtree-city`) links down
  to the three product pages; each product page links back up to the hub and across
  to the other two siblings.
- **Title tags ≤ 60 chars, meta descriptions ≤ 155 chars** (targets, not hard limits).

## 3. Two things to raise with Reachgorilla

**oldhousetonewhome.net** — on the sheet twice at $442.14. The same domain was
billed twice to ngwindows.com with no findable live link. Demand the live URLs
before paying.

**Compliance.** nangwizard.net sells nitrous oxide chargers. UK recreational
supply has been Class C under the Misuse of Drugs Act since Nov 2023. UK
publishers (dorsetecho.co.uk, bmmagazine.co.uk, fiftyandfab.co.uk,
moneymagpie.com) will either refuse the brief or run it as a drugs-news angle —
which is a mention, not a commercial link. Buy US food/home blogs instead.

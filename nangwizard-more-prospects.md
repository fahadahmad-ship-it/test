## Verification — stronger than my first pass

My original nangwizard check relied on transcribing Ahrefs pages by hand, and I later found a
transcription gap doing the same thing on another account. These 30 were verified with the
regex method instead: a filter run directly against Ahrefs **all-time** referring domains
(2,850 for this site), so nothing depends on my copying a list correctly.

I also control-tested the Semrush filter on this target. `worleygig.com` returned two live
dofollow links, anchor "Best Whip Cream Chargers", from a December 2022 post about whipped
cream chargers — so the empty results for the picks below are real negatives, not a broken query.

**All 30: zero matches in Ahrefs all-time, Semrush, or your sheet.**

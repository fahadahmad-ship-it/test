# nangwizard.net — ipsnews.net verdict + 29 more

## ipsnews.net — not built, but I would not buy it

**Not built for nangwizard.** Verified against Ahrefs all-time referring domains (2,850) with a
substring match — zero rows. It *is* built for **bluegoatcyber.com** (their September '26 sheet),
so it is inventory this agency already uses.

Sheet metrics: **$50, AS 37, TF 64, 4,359 traffic, 37,987 referring domains.**

TF 64 for $50 looks outstanding. The problem is the ratio:

**37,987 referring domains against 4,359 monthly visits = 8,715 RD per 1,000 visits.**

I ranked every site in the 13,405-row sheet on that measure. **ipsnews.net is #39 out of
12,935** — the 0.3rd percentile.

For scale, here is how it compares to the two cases where I argued this heuristic produces
false positives and reinstated the site:

| Domain | RD/1k | My verdict |
|---|---|---|
| infosecurity-magazine.com | 1,327 | Genuine trade press — reinstated |
| medcitynews.com | 1,733 | Genuine trade press — reinstated |
| **ipsnews.net** | **8,715** | **5–6× worse** |

Inter Press Service is a real news agency, and the TF 64 and 37,987 referring domains are
genuinely earned over decades of journalism. But 4,359 monthly visits means it is effectively
dormant as a destination while still linking out at enormous volume. You would be buying a
historical trust score attached to a page almost nobody reads, on a domain widely used as a
paid-placement channel.

At $50 it is not expensive enough to be a disaster. It is simply not what the TF 64 suggests.
`thedyrt.com` at $50 gives you AS 52 and 305,155 real visits instead.

## Critical read
- Spend the budget on ANGLE 1. A /franchise domain is selling a $100k+
  business opportunity; a link from a business/investor site reaches the
  buyer. A senior-care consumer link reaches the wrong person (the care
  recipient's family), however on-topic "home care" sounds.
- Best single buys: educba ($56) + bbntimes ($130) + womenentrepreneurs-
  review ($100) for the franchise angle; seniorliving.com ($180) as the
  one consumer-side brand link; wvgazettemail/rutlandherald for local.
- Do NOT over-index on health-keyword sites — most reach care consumers
  or clinicians, not franchise investors.
- Re-verify caringbridge is a buyable editorial placement, not a profile.

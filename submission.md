# Link Building Direction: Priority Inner Pages

**Client:** accessible.org
**Market:** United States, organic search
**Data source:** Ahrefs, September 2026

## Overview

This document sets out a focused link building direction for one to two priority
inner pages. It covers target page selection, the recommended anchor direction,
and the source types that deliver value for both AI citation and traditional SEO.
The intent is a small, high leverage direction rather than a full campaign plan.

## Target Page Selection

Two pages are recommended, each serving a different objective.

The primary target for traditional SEO is the ADA website audits service page at
`accessible.org/services/ada-website-audits/`. It already ranks position 8 for
"ada compliance audit," a commercial term with a keyword difficulty of only 2 and
roughly 1,100 monthly searches, yet it remains under linked. That combination
makes it the clearest opportunity on the site. A modest volume of relevant links,
supported by internal linking, should be enough to move it into the top 3 on a
page that drives revenue. The alternative audits page was not chosen because it
already ranks in the top 3 for its terms and sits close to its ceiling, so
additional links would return little.

The secondary target for AI citation is the digital accessibility companies page
at `accessible.org/digital-accessibility-companies/`. Its comparison style format
is what AI systems retrieve and quote for queries such as "top digital
accessibility companies." The page earns no organic traffic today, so it
represents pure upside, and it addresses the AI citation objective that the
commercial audit page cannot.

## Recommended Anchor Direction

The profile should remain predominantly branded and partial match, with exact
match kept minimal and introduced last. The domain currently holds very few
commercial anchors, so a concentration of exact match anchors on a low difficulty
term would appear unnatural and invite scrutiny.

For the audit page, the anchor mix should be:

- Branded: Accessible.org
- Partial match: ADA website audit, ADA website audit services
- Topical: web accessibility audit, WCAG audit
- Exact match: ada compliance audit, capped at roughly 10 percent and delivered
  mainly through internal links to avoid an overoptimization footprint

For the companies page, the anchors should stay descriptive and non commercial,
since the goal is to be cited as a neutral authority:

- Branded: Accessible.org
- Descriptive: digital accessibility companies, top digital accessibility
  companies

## Source Types

For traditional SEO value, the priority is topically relevant editorial links,
where relevance and editorial context matter far more than raw domain rating.
Strong fits include:

- Legal and compliance blogs, web development and agency sites, and B2B SaaS
  publications
- Agency resource pages
- Journalist and expert commentary requests on ADA lawsuit and compliance stories

For AI citation value, the priority is becoming the source that gets quoted. AI
systems consistently favour material that is original, well referenced, and easy
to cite. The strongest sources here are:

- Industry roundups and comparison articles
- References from Wikipedia and Wikidata
- Original data and statistics assets, such as the settlement amounts page and
  the automated scans study
- Mentions in directories and relevant forum discussions

## Additional Recommendations

Two issues should be addressed alongside the link building work. First, the
current backlink profile contains PBN and testimonial spam, which warrants a
disavow review. Second, audit intent is presently split across four competing
URLs, and these should be consolidated into a single canonical page before links
are directed to the target.

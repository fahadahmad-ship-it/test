# nangwizard.net — sub-$100, second pass

My first sub-$100 list was thin. I went back to the raw sheet and scanned the whole band on
quality first (AS ≥ 32, TF ≥ 20, traffic ≥ 15,000) rather than filtering on topic first.
That surfaced 121 candidates — and explained why the band looked so poor.

AS 84 for $50 is not a bargain — it is a user profile or project page on a platform. The score
belongs to Letterboxd, not to your link. Every one of these is excluded.

Strip the platforms out and the editorial remainder is mostly off-topic: football
(`onevalefan.co.uk`, `maltafootball.com`, `persianleague.com`), gaming (`thesims3.com`,
`culture-games.com`), mobile phones, cars, and Ukrainian, Moroccan and Italian local news.

## What I would do

$50 more per link moves you from "thin blogs and platform profiles" to "national newspaper".
If the cap is genuinely hard, take `thedyrt.com` $50 and `filmibeat.com` $60 for traffic and
`thechocolatelife.com` $100 for topic. If there is any flex at all, `theglobeandmail.com` at
$150 is the buy.

Metrics are the marketplace sheet's own figures, not re-verified per domain; I contacted no
publisher.

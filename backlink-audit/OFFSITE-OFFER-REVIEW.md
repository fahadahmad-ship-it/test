# Review — "Performance Lab Off-site Campaign Direction" ($4,000, The Search Initiative)

Checked against live Ahrefs and Semrush data, 2026-08-27. The proposal quotes
Semrush figures, so Semrush is the reference below; where Ahrefs disagrees
materially it is noted.

**Short answer to the two questions.** The important pages are *mostly* there —
3 of the 4 target URLs are correct and well chosen, 1 is wrong, and several of
the site's strongest commercial pages are missing. **Anchor text is not in the
document at all**, which for a 7-link order at $550 a link is the single
biggest gap: anchors are what decide whether the links move the target terms.

---

## 1. The target pages

The document commits to four URLs (§ "Target pages").

|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/blogs/sleep/best-magnesium-for-sleep` | Correct. #7 for "best magnesium for sleep" (22,200) |
| 2 | `/blogs/sleep/magnesium-bisglycinate-vs-glycinate` | Correct. #13 for "magnesium bisglycinate" (22,200) |
| 3 | `/blogs/multi/best-multivitamin-for-men` | Correct. #23 / #14 on the head terms |
| 4 | `/blogs/pre-lab-pro/creatine-before-or-after-workout` | **Wrong — truncated** |

### The creatine URL is incomplete

Every ranking row in both tools points at:

```
/blogs/pre-lab-pro/creatine-before-or-after-workout-when-should-you-take-it
```

The document drops `-when-should-you-take-it`. Across ~30 Semrush rows and
several Ahrefs rows the short form never appears. It is very likely a 404 or a
redirect. **Confirm the exact URL before any placement goes live** — a link
built to a 404 is $550 wasted, and one built to a redirect leaks part of its
value.

### Pages that should arguably be on the list

The proposal targets blog pages only and skips the two collection pages that
already rank *and* convert:

| Page | Monthly traffic | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| `/Magnesium` | 1,221 | #3 "magnesium malate benefits" — a commercial collection page already ranking |
| `/Sleep` | 792 | #7 "what magnesium is best for sleep" (10,000) — competes with the blog page for the same term |
| `/blogs/magnesium/magnesium-malate-vs-glycinate` | 1,629 | **#1** for "magnesium malate benefits" (9,900), 811 traffic from that term alone |
| `/blogs/magnesium/magnesium-taurate-vs-glycinate` | 990 | #4 "magnesium taurate benefits" (4,400) |

Worth raising with them: `/Sleep` and `/blogs/sleep/best-magnesium-for-sleep`
both rank for "what magnesium is best for sleep". Pushing links at the blog page
without addressing that overlap may just move the cannibalisation around.

## 2. Anchor text — absent, and the most important omission

The document names four URLs and **zero anchors**. That is the variable that
decides whether a link moves "best multivitamin for men" or just adds authority
in the abstract, and it is also the main risk vector.

### The good news: there is a lot of headroom

The current anchor profile is overwhelmingly branded and bare-URL:

| Anchor | Referring domains |
|---|---|
| `performancelab.com` | 781 |
| *(empty / image)* | 392 |
| `Performance Lab` | 273 |
| `performancelab` | 247 |
| `www.performancelab.com` | 88 |
| `here` / `website` / `click here` / `read more` | 78 / 78 / 67 / 66 |

Descriptive commercial anchors are essentially absent — nothing resembling
"best magnesium for sleep" or "best multivitamin for men" appears anywhere in
the top 25. With a profile this branded, seven descriptive anchors carry
**negligible over-optimisation risk** and are the highest-leverage thing the
campaign can do.

### Proposed anchors

Ask them to commit to these in writing before ordering. One exact-match per
cluster at most; the rest partial or natural.

**Magnesium & Sleep — 3 links**

| Target | Anchor | Type |
|---|---|---|
| `best-magnesium-for-sleep` | best magnesium for sleep | Exact — use once |
| `best-magnesium-for-sleep` | which magnesium is best for sleep | Partial |
| `magnesium-bisglycinate-vs-glycinate` | magnesium bisglycinate vs glycinate | Partial |

**Multivitamin — 3 links**

| Target | Anchor | Type |
|---|---|---|
| `best-multivitamin-for-men` | best multivitamin for men | Exact — use once |
| `best-multivitamin-for-men` | best men's multivitamin | Partial |
| `best-multivitamin-for-men` | choosing a men's multivitamin | Natural phrase |

**Creatine — 1 link**

| Target | Anchor | Type |
|---|---|---|
| `creatine-before-or-after-workout-when-should-you-take-it` | creatine before or after a workout | Partial |

Reserve fallbacks if a publisher will not take a commercial anchor:
"Performance Lab's guide to magnesium and sleep", "research from Performance
Lab", "Performance Lab NutriGenesis Multi".

## 3. Factual accuracy of the proposal

Mostly sound. The positions drift a little and all but one drift in the
client's favour.

| Claim | Actual (Semrush) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| best magnesium for sleep 22,200 · #10 | 22,200 · **#7** | Understated |
| which magnesium is best for sleep 22,200 · #6 | 22,200 · #5 | Fine |
| magnesium bisglycinate 22,200 · #9 | 22,200 · **#13** | Overstated |
| what magnesium is best for sleep 9,900 · #4 | 9,900 · #5 | Fine |
| best multivitamin for men 22,200 · #21 | 22,200 · #23 | Fine |
| best men's multivitamin 12,100 · #13 | 12,100 · #14 | Fine |
| creatine before or after workout 18,100 · #11 | 18,100 · **#14** | Overstated |
| when to take creatine 27,100 · #27 | 27,100 · #25 | Fine |
| Product pages ~1,000 visits | ~1,214 | Fine |
| "NutriGenesis Multi Men is your 2nd-strongest product page" | It is **1st** (201 vs 198) | Understated |
| rear delt fly 74,000 · #4 | **84,000 · #3**, 5,098 visits/mo | Stale |

Two things to push back on:

**The multivitamin cluster is harder than the document implies.** It calls
positions 13–21 "credible to Google but under-supported — precisely the
situation that authority solves". Every head term in that cluster has a Semrush
competitive density of **1.00**, the maximum, and the SERPs are Healthline,
Forbes and similar. Three links at $550 is a reasonable bet, not the
straightforward fix the wording suggests. Ask what movement they will commit to.

**Dismissing "rear delt fly" is too quick.** The document says it will not chase
it because "there is no product at the end of that search". Fair on intent — but
that page pulls **5,098 visits a month, more than double any other page on the
site**, and roughly $905/mo in equivalent traffic value. It costs nothing to
route its internal links into the Sleep and Multi money pages. Not a link target;
definitely an asset.

## 4. Two things to settle before signing

**Submit the disavow first.** The proposal makes no mention of the link profile
it is building on top of. We have just identified **1,183 toxic referring
domains**, including live link-vendor injections. Buying seven links into a
profile carrying that is spending money on the accelerator while the handbrake
is on.

**The affiliate leak is bigger than the audit sample showed.** Ahrefs' full
index shows the anchor
`https://www.performancelab.com/?a_aid=68990cbe508aa&a_bid=a2ad38c1` on **223
referring domains with 862 dofollow links** — one affiliate ID, spammed across
223 sites. Our 50k sample showed 24 domains; the real figure is nearly ten times
that. This is an affiliate-programme compliance matter, not a disavow one, and
it is worth more than the $4,000 campaign.

For context on the vendor standard: the same index shows 221 referring domains
carrying a spun testimonial for `SEOExpress.org`, and 234 carrying
`TELEGRAM @SEO_ANOMALY - SEO BACKLINKS`. Someone has sold this site links
before. The proposal's $550 floor and "real, indexable, editorially reviewed
sites" language is the right commitment — get it in writing, with the publisher
list approved in advance rather than reported afterwards.

---

## Verdict

The strategy is sound and the reasoning behind targeting blog pages over product
pages is correct — product pages really are ~1.2% of organic traffic, and the
document is right that a homepage-and-product-page campaign would look tidy and
do little.

Before signing, get three things fixed:

1. The **creatine URL** corrected and verified.
2. **Anchor text specified in writing** for all seven placements.
3. The **disavow submitted** first, and the affiliate ID `68990cbe508aa`
   investigated.

And ask them to add `/Magnesium`, `/Sleep` or `magnesium-malate-vs-glycinate` to
the target list, or explain why blog pages alone are the better bet.

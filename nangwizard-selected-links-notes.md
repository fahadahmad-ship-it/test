## Cautions before purchase
- **Verify each live** — vendor AS/TF/traffic are self-reported; spot-check in Ahrefs and confirm the site still publishes real editorial (not a dropped/spammed domain).
- **Geographic/topical dilution:** several high-AS options are India/Nigeria/Cyprus news farms (indiatoday, punchng, thisdaylive, deccanchronicle, thesun.ng). Fine occasionally for DR, but keep the *majority* AU/food/lifestyle for relevance — the profile is already heavy on off-topic magazine links.
- **Anchor mix:** per the link plan, keep new links mostly branded/naked/partial; reserve exact-match ("nangs delivery", "cream chargers") for a few high-trust food/AU placements only.
- **Skip from the sheet:** finance/crypto/forex, pure sports, agriculture/farming, gaming-only, religion, and the .EDU eportfolio links (canvas.*.edu "eportfolios" are a manipulated/greyhat footprint — not worth the risk for this brand).
- **Already-built (do NOT re-buy):** the list included domains Nang Wizard already has (e.g. gigwise.com, applemagazine.com, ediblemanhattan.com, we-heart.com, urbansplatter.com, thecanary.co, and others) — these were removed from the picks.

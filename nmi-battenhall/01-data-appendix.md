**This is the single most important finding in the analysis.** Page-level referring domains on
winning pages are in the **single to low double digits**. Domain Rating across both SERPs runs
**74–99**. Ranking here is bought with *domain* authority and topical relevance, not with volume
of links to the target page.

Practical bar: **8–15 quality, relevant referring domains per target page** is sufficient to
out-link almost every page on these SERPs. NMI's DR 77 already clears the domain bar for
`embedded payments` and sits at the low end for `payment processing`.

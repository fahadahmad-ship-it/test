A quarter of your link profile is spam, and it is still growing.

Roughly 1,170 referring domains carry a spam flag, with around 170 more arriving every month and nobody watching. Against that, NMI already outperforms its named competitors on the links it does have. This is where the opportunity is, and the kind of links we would build.

NMI reaches 46 percent of Authorize.Net's US traffic and 82 percent of its keyword footprint from one eighth of the referring domains.

And momentum favours you. Over the last twelve months NMI added 113 referring domains a month. Authorize.Net lost 315 a month and is down 3,780 on the year.

Two things worth saying plainly

The Authority Score gap is wider than it looks. Three points between 46 and 49 is not three units of work. The scale is logarithmic, so it stands for thousands of referring domains and each further point costs more than the last. The score alone makes the distance look far shorter than it is.

The backlink count is misleading. NMI shows 22.8 million backlinks from fewer than 5,000 websites. The NMI badge sits on merchant checkout pages, so it is counted again on every page it appears on. To a search engine, one website linking a thousand times is still one website. Adyen averages 24 links per website. NMI averages 4,605. Every figure in this document counts websites, not links.

02  What we found

A spam network nobody is watching.

It would pass any check built on authority alone. It fails the moment you look at traffic, which is exactly how we screen.

Arriving at roughly 170 domains a month, with 72 percent of it landed in 2026 alone. Around 95 percent points at the homepage. Three clusters, none of them subtle: domains that openly sell links, running between Domain Rating 53 and 73 with no traffic at all; a single network of more than 100 domains on one commercial top level domain, every one at Domain Rating 32 with no traffic; and a directory network between 46 and 54. The anchor text has moved through five generations since October 2025, each a different link selling brand.

A filter set on authority alone passes all 327 of these domains. Adding a traffic threshold removes almost all of them.

This is the whole reason we screen on traffic and relevance first, and treat authority score as the last check rather than the first.

For balance, your good links are genuinely good

783 referring domains carry a thousand or more monthly organic visits, including Wikipedia, Adobe, GitHub, Forbes, Shopify, Salesforce, Bloomberg, Mastercard and PYMNTS. Roughly one in five. There are simply not many of them, and almost all point at the homepage rather than at anything that sells.

We have not assessed penalty risk and are not claiming any. Establishing whether the spam is doing measurable harm is part of a full backlink audit, which would precede any delivery schedule.

03  The link plan

Fewer links, genuinely good ones.

Every target is a site a direct competitor already links from and NMI does not, screened on real traffic and topical fit. No marketplace filler, no names a technical reader would recognise as bought.

The very largest titles, Reuters and Mashable among them, feature as periodic showpieces rather than every month, since one placement there is worth several elsewhere. And the genuine payments trade press, American Banker, Payments Dive and Finextra, does not sell placements at all and is earned through digital PR on original data. Those are targets, not purchases, and we would build toward them.

Two plans

The same quality bar in both, and neither drops to marketplace filler. The difference is how many placements a month and how often a flagship national title features, never the standard of the sites. We would rather place fewer links NMI is proud to show than more it would not.

A month that features a national title carries one or two fewer placements, because one genuine national name is worth more to a payments brand than several mid tier ones. The floor never moves.

What every domain has to clear

Real organic traffic weighted to the United States, a topical fit with payments, fintech or ecommerce, a followed in content link, a clean outbound profile and Authority Score 30 as a floor. Traffic is the first gate, not the last, because it is the one that catches the spam network in section 02. We do not use private blog networks, thin directories, sitewide or footer placements, or any site without real traffic whatever its score reads.

A full backlink audit precedes the delivery schedule. This document sets the strategy and the kind of links. The audit turns it into a month by month plan.

# NMI — Backlink Snapshot
### Prepared by SUSO Digital for Battenhall

*A high-level read of NMI's link profile against Stripe, Adyen and Authorize.Net. All metrics are
Semrush. This is a snapshot, not an audit — a full backlink audit is a separate engagement.*

---

## 1. Where NMI stands

| Domain | Authority Score | Referring domains |
|---|---|---|
| Stripe | 79 | 696,910 |
| **Adyen** | **49** | 59,017 |
| **Authorize.Net** | **48** | 39,946 |
| **NMI** | **46** | **4,968** |

**NMI is within 2–3 Authority Score points of both Adyen and Authorize.Net.** That is a closable
gap, and it is the single most important fact in this snapshot.

The referring domain count is where the distance is real: NMI has **4,968 referring domains against
Adyen's 59,017 and Authorize.Net's 39,946** — 8x and 12x behind respectively.

Stripe is not a competitive target for a programme at this budget, and we would not scope against it.

---

## 2. Volume is not the problem — quality concentration is

Of NMI's **4,968 referring domains, 374 are Authority Score 40 or above.** That is 7.8%.

Benchmarked against the closest comparable profile in the category, NMI needs roughly **2.7x more
authority-grade referring domains** to match. That multiple is consistent at every quality
threshold we tested, which makes it a reliable basis for target-setting.

The headline backlink number tells a different and misleading story. NMI's profile carries an
extremely high ratio of backlinks to referring domains, driven by a single recurring integration
placement. It inflates the count without contributing authority. **We would not count these toward
any target, and we would advise Battenhall not to report on total backlinks for this account.**

---

## 3. Two things we flagged

**🚩 A third-party spam anchor is live on the profile.**
An anchor advertising a black-hat link service via Telegram appears across **100 referring
domains**, first seen October 2025 and still active. NMI did not place these. At current volume
the risk is low, but it is unmonitored and it is growing.

**🚩 A cluster of misspelled brand-variant anchors requires ownership verification.**
Roughly a dozen typo-variants of NMI's acquired brands (USAePay, Network Merchants) appear with
suspiciously uniform link volumes. These are either NMI's own defensive registrations or a
third-party network. We can tell which, but it needs the ownership check.

Neither is urgent. Both are the kind of thing that goes unnoticed until it matters.

---

## 4. The gap nobody is covering

NMI sells embedded payments to **US developers**. Adyen, Authorize.Net, Checkout.com and Paysafe
all hold links from the developer ecosystem — package registries, integration directories,
technology profilers and developer communities.

**NMI holds none of them.**

Sample of domains linking to all four competitors and not to NMI:

| Domain | AS | Type |
|---|---|---|
| CocoaPods | 46 | Developer package registry |
| IndieHackers | 54 | Developer community |
| Wappalyzer | 42 | Technology profiler |
| RetailDive | 55 | Trade press |

*(This is four of a much longer list.)*

This is a link category that maps exactly onto NMI's ideal customer, is largely free to acquire,
and delivers qualified referral traffic alongside authority. It is the fastest available win in
the profile and it is currently unexploited.

---

## 5. What we would build

| Priority | Link type | Target AS |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fintech and payments trade press | 30–60 |
| 2 | Developer ecosystem and integration listings | 30–55 |
| 3 | Comparison, review and software directories | 25–50 |
| 4 | Data-led digital PR — original survey data | 50+ |

**Quality floor: Authority Score 30 minimum, with the majority at AS 40+.** Below AS 30 a link
does not move the metric Battenhall reports on, and we would not place it.

On the fourth: NMI's own content history shows what earns links in this category. Their
generative-AI-in-payments piece earned 143 referring domains and their consumer survey earned 42.
Original data is the format that works for this brand — and it gives Battenhall a PR cycle to run
alongside the link programme rather than in competition with it.

---

## 6. Where we would point the links

Our analysis found **eight NMI pages sitting at positions 2–12 on commercially valuable terms,
held up by an average of 4.6 referring domains each.** One of them ranks 5th on a term worth
~$10,000/month in equivalent paid traffic — on a single referring domain.

The link plan leads with these, with a lighter layer to the product pages. We will walk through
the full target page map on the call.

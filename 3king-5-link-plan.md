Both domains funnel to the same destination. **The "brand hijack" is almost

**3king-game-mobile.com has DR 3.5 and outranks 3king.cc (DR 19) across the

## PART 5 — THE HONEST CAVEAT

**These 5 links will not move 19 visits/month, and nobody should be told
otherwise.**

The evidence is unambiguous: 2,683 referring domains produced DR 19 and zero
non-brand rankings; the clone wins the brand SERP at DR 3.5 with no link equity
at all. Links are not the binding variable in this SERP.

Four actions outrank all 5 links on expected impact, and three cost nothing:

| Rank | Action | Cost | Why it beats the links |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Establish who controls 3king-game-mobile.com** | Zero — one email | If it's the same operator, the "hijack" is an internal contest and the fix is a conversation, not a campaign |
| **2** | **Pick ONE canonical domain and 301 the satellites** | Low | Nine domains splitting one entity is why a DR 56 domain ranks for nothing. Evidence favours **3king.win** as canonical, not 3king.cc |
| **3** | **Ship app-store + entity signals** | Low | `ica.net.vn` ranks #2 for `bắn cá online` at **DR 10** on app-entity strength alone. 3king.win's genuine historical anchors are literally "Android download" / "iOS download" |
| **4** | **Kill the dead tail; link the orphaned content** | Zero | ~25 UR-0 pages absorbing internal PageRank; ~41 real pages with zero links |
| 5 | The 5 links above | — | Lowest expected impact of anything on this list |

**No trademark or legal action.** The outbound-link evidence indicates this is
not an unauthorised clone. A takedown against your own partner is an own goal.

### The question that has to be answered first

**Does the client control 3king-game-mobile.com, and is 3king.cc or 3king.win
meant to be the primary property?**

The answer changes the plan materially:
- *If same owner* — stop the internal contest; the brand SERP is already won by
  the portfolio, and all 5 links go to non-brand acquisition on the canonical domain.
- *If different owner* — link 3 becomes genuinely contested and the entity work
  in Part 5 becomes urgent rather than merely high-value.

Until that is answered, links 1, 2 and 4 are safe to place now — they target
validated non-brand commercial terms on existing pages and are not wasted under
either scenario. **I'd place those three and hold links 3 and 5.**

## 1. Read this first — two findings

**A. Domain Rating is only 27** (Ahrefs), Ahrefs Rank ~5.4M. This is a *weak* profile for its age — most of the "referring domains" are junk (see B). The real, legitimate link count is small. So this campaign is **foundation-building**: prioritise genuinely relevant senior-care/franchise links over volume, and expect DR to move quickly off a low base.

**B. 🚨 Massive negative-SEO / spam footprint — audit & disavow first.** The referring-domain profile is dominated by the exact same black-hat network seen on your other clients:
- **SEOExpress** cluster — dozens of `*-seoexpress.store` / `seoexpress.shop/.site/.online` domains and testimonial spam.
- **"link-baron" / "outrank-hq" / "rank-forge"** `.store` PBN domains (100+).
- **`*.shop` rank/seo spam** (`rankseo*`, `siterank*`, `googleseo*`, `backlink*`), plus `rankyour.website`, `buybacklinks.agency`, `backlinkshop.site`, and a block of `374861xx.xyz` domains.

**Action:** export the full backlink profile, isolate these clusters + `is_spam=true`, and submit a **domain-level disavow** in Google Search Console *before* spending on new links. On a DR-27 domain this spam is a real liability, not just noise.

**C. Already-built accuracy caveat (important).** Ahrefs and Semrush both miss links placed in the last ~1–3 months. I've excluded everything in the current index, but **the only reliable "already-built" gate is your own placement sheet.** If you have a Nurse Next Door placement/outreach sheet, send it and I'll re-filter so we don't double-buy.

| Anchor type | Share | Examples |

| Generic | ~15% | "learn more", "this franchise opportunity" |
| Partial / topical | ~15% | "senior home care franchise", "home care business opportunity" |
| Exact-match | **≤5%** | "home care franchise" — high-trust editorial only |

**Target pages:** point Tier-1 links at the franchise/opportunity and territory pages; Tier-2 (healthcare) links at any care-model / about pages; Tier-3 at the main franchise landing page. Spread across pages — don't blast the homepage.

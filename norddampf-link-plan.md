## 3. Problems to fix first (critical)

### 3a. 🚨 Negative-SEO / toxic-link disavow — TOP PRIORITY
The scale here is worse than a normal profile: **~180k all-time backlinks** and multiple black-hat anchor clusters (Telegram spam services, SEOExpress testimonial, "DA 50 PA 40 PBN", darksidelinks). Spam-flagged referring domains include: `rankyour.website`, `backlinker.shop`, `buybacklinks.agency`, `rank-top.click`, `suchnadel.de`, `webspider24.de`, `backlinkshop.site`, `gelbeseiten.de`.

**Action:** Export the full backlink profile, isolate `is_spam=true` + the Telegram/SEOExpress/PBN anchor clusters, and submit a **domain-level disavow** in Google Search Console. Given the volume, this is likely an active negative-SEO attack — monitor monthly and top up the disavow. **Do this before spending on new links.**

### 3b. Anchor over-optimisation
"Vaporizer" (13 RD) + "Vaporizer kaufen" + "Vaporizer von Norddampf" are a heavy exact-match commercial cluster on a mid-DR domain. New links must skew branded/partial/natural (see §6).

### 3c. Relevance & language
Some links are generic German PR/press-release syndication with little topical relevance. The domain's audience is **German vape/cannabis consumers** — future links should be **German-language and niche-relevant** (vaporizer/cannabis/lifestyle/gadget), not broad business-PR filler.

## 5. Link opportunity list (prioritised)

### Tier 1 — German vape / vaporizer / cannabis niche (highest relevance)
- **Vaporizer review & community sites:** verdampftnochmal.de (forum), vapowelt/vaping-lee-style blogs, German gadget/tech-review sites that cover vaporizers, YouTube/blog device reviews (send RELICT/Hammah units for honest reviews → review links).
- **Cannabis/hemp culture (post-legalisation):** hanfverband.de (have — get more), German cannabis magazines/blogs, Hanf-focused news, CSC (Cannabis Social Club) resources, grow blogs. Germany's 2024 legalisation created a wave of linkable cannabis-lifestyle content.
- **Headshop / grow / lifestyle directories** (German).

### Tier 2 — German digital PR & linkable assets
- **Product-led PR:** RELICT/Hammah launch angles, "Vaporizer vs. Rauchen" health-angle explainers, temperature/terpene guides — pitch to German lifestyle/health/gadget media.
- **Data/guides:** German "Vaporizer kaufen" buyer's guide, legal-status explainer (cannabis law in Germany, by state) — a durable, citable asset.
- **HARO-DE equivalents:** journalist-request platforms (e.g. #Journalistenanfragen on social, dpa-relevant, German HARO-style services) — earn editorial mentions as a vaporizer expert.

### Tier 3 — Authority profiles & citations (durable, branded anchors)
- German business/review profiles: ProvenExpert (have), Trustpilot/Trusted Shops (critical for German e-commerce trust), Gelbe Seiten (legit listing), Yelp.de, local listings.
- International brand profiles: about.me, Crunchbase, LinkedIn company, relevant marketplaces.

### Tier 4 — International (secondary)
- Selective English vape/cannabis/lifestyle guest posts (you already have offthemrkt, yourhealthmagazine, growweedeasy) — fine for DR but keep the **majority German** for relevance.

### Avoid / do NOT do
- ❌ Any Telegram/PBN/"buy backlinks" service — already a severe liability; more will compound risk.
- ❌ Generic non-German press-release syndication with no topical fit.
- ❌ New exact-match "Vaporizer" / "Vaporizer kaufen" anchors at scale.
- ⚠️ **Compliance:** keep messaging on the **device / aromatherapy / dry-herb** framing and adhere to German advertising rules for vaping/cannabis-adjacent products; the legal explainer doubles as your best linkable asset and a compliance shield.

## 7. 90-day roadmap

**Month 1 — Clean up (urgent)**
- Full backlink audit + **disavow** the negative-SEO clusters (~150+ RD of Telegram/SEOExpress/PBN spam). Set monthly monitoring.
- Set up/optimise Trusted Shops, Trustpilot, ProvenExpert (German trust signals).

**Month 2 — Niche relevance**
- Seed 5–8 **German vaporizer/cannabis** review & blog placements (incl. sending devices for reviews).
- Pursue verdampftnochmal.de-type community presence and cannabis-culture links.
- Begin journalist-request (HARO-DE) responses.

**Month 3 — PR asset**
- Publish German buyer's guide + cannabis-law explainer; pitch to German lifestyle/gadget/health media.
- 1 product-led PR push (RELICT/Hammah) with branded anchors.

**KPIs:** disavow live + toxic RD trending down; DR 40 → 45+; **relevant German** referring domains up; exact-match anchor share down; movement on "Vaporizer"/"Vaporizer kaufen" and product terms vs 157grad.de.

---

## 8. Strategic summary

1. **Disavow first — this is urgent.** ~180k all-time backlinks and active Telegram/PBN spam make cleanup the #1 job, ahead of acquisition.
2. **Relevance + German language beat volume.** Close the 157grad.de gap with vaporizer/cannabis-niche, German-language links, not generic PR.
3. **Fix the anchor mix** — dilute "Vaporizer"/"Vaporizer kaufen".
4. **Use the 2024 legalisation tailwind** — cannabis-culture and legal-explainer content is highly linkable right now in Germany.
5. **Lead with product reviews + a buyer's guide/legal asset** — the natural, durable, relevant link sources for this brand.

## 3. Problems to fix first (critical)

### 3a. Large negative-SEO / spam footprint — audit & disavow
The same spam patterns seen across this vendor's clients are present and sizeable:

- **SEOExpress.org testimonial spam** — ~165 referring domains carry a spun *"Running bluegoatcyber.com, we once thought high-ranking keywords were unreachable… collaboration with SEOExpress.org…"* anchor (all nofollow, DR≤41). Third-party vendor using the brand as a testimonial prop.
- **Telegram SEO-spam anchors** — *"TELEGRAM @SEO_ANOMALY – SEO BACKLINKS, BLACK-LINKS…"* (150 ref domains, 508 links) and *"TG @BHS_LINKS…"* (36). Classic toxic/black-hat spam.
- **PBN "DA 50 PA 40 Premium PBN Network" anchor** (26 ref domains) and a *"Complete SEO for bluegoatcyber.com…"* vendor anchor (20 ref domains, DR 0.8).
- **Spam-flagged domains** (e.g. rankyour.website) to disavow.

**This is the #1 priority.** That's ~370+ referring domains of manipulative/toxic anchors. Export the full backlink set, isolate these anchor clusters + `is_spam=true`, and submit a **domain-level disavow** in Google Search Console. For a compliance-sensitive B2B brand, a clean profile also matters for reputation, not just rankings.

### 3b. Anchor over-optimisation
"medical device cybersecurity" (131 RD) + the other exact-match commercial anchors are over-weighted. Cap new exact-match; push branded/partial/natural (see §6).

### 3c. Relevance dilution from generic PR
A large share of links are **UK regional newspapers** (liverpoolecho, yorkshirepost, walesonline, bristolpost, devonlive, portsmouth.co.uk, plymouthherald, thestar.co.uk, etc.) — the same syndication network used for unrelated clients. For a **US medical-device-cybersecurity** firm these are low-relevance filler. They inflate DR but do little for topical authority or the buyer audience. Future budget should shift to MedTech/healthcare/security-industry placements.

## 4. Competitive & relevance gap

Organic "competitors" are dominated by **standards/regulatory bodies** — fda.gov, iso.org, ansi.org, aami.org — plus **johner-institute.com** (DR51, medical-device regulatory consultancy, the closest true peer), **blackduck.com** (DR81, software security), radsource.us, iteh.ai. This confirms the niche is **compliance-content-led**: you rank by being the authoritative explainer on medical-device security standards.

**Reading:** the ranking lever here is **topical authority + genuine industry citations**, not link volume. Blue Goat already has the DR; it lacks enough **relevant** links pointing at its compliance/blog assets to push them from page 2–3 to page 1 for terms like *medical device testing* (1,200 vol, pos 24), *radiology information system* (700, pos 25), *medical device penetration testing* (200, pos 10).

## 5. Link opportunity list (prioritised)

### Tier 1 — Industry-relevant editorial & thought leadership (the real lever)
Target MedTech, healthcare-IT, and cybersecurity publications where the audience and topical relevance are real:
- **MedTech / medical-device media:** MedTech Dive, MassDevice, Medical Device and Diagnostic Industry (MD+DI), Medical Design & Outsourcing, MedCity News, Medical Device Network, RAPS Regulatory Focus (raps.org — already have; get more), AAMI (aami.org).
- **Healthcare-IT / health security:** HealthITSecurity, HealthcareITNews, Healthcare IT Today, Fierce Healthcare, HIMSS, Journal of AHIMA.
- **Cybersecurity trade press:** already have BleepingComputer, SecureWorld, SpiceWorks, CSIS — pursue Dark Reading, SC Media, The Hacker News, Help Net Security, Infosecurity Magazine, CSO Online, Security Boulevard.
- **Method:** contributed articles / expert bylines on FDA cybersecurity guidance, SBOMs for medical devices, pen-testing connected devices, IEC 62304 / 21 CFR 820 explainers — mapped to your existing blog assets.

### Tier 2 — Digital PR / expert commentary (earns natural, diverse anchors)
- **HARO / Qwoted / SourceBottle / Featured** — respond as a medical-device-security SME. High-authority, editorially-earned links with natural anchors; ideal for diluting the exact-match ratio.
- **Data/research asset:** publish an annual "Medical Device Cybersecurity / FDA premarket findings" report or vulnerability-trend study → the kind of citable asset MedTech and security journalists link to (mirrors how csis.org, mdpi already reference you).
- **Newsjacking** FDA guidance updates (e.g. Section 524B / refuse-to-accept) with rapid expert explainers.

### Tier 3 — Podcast & partnership expansion (you already do this well)
- Keep syndicating **The Med Device Cyber Podcast**; pursue **guest appearances** on MedTech/security podcasts (each earns a show-notes link + audience).
- Partner/mutual-resource links with complementary (non-competing) vendors: QMS/regulatory consultancies, medical-device test labs, SBOM tooling, healthcare compliance firms.

### Tier 4 — Authority profiles, directories & academic citations
- **B2B/service directories (relevant):** Clutch, G2, GoodFirms (have), UpCity, TechBehemoths, The Manifest, Cybersecurity-specific directories (e.g. Cybersecurity Excellence, MSSP/service listings).
- **Academic / standards / .gov-adjacent:** you already have odu.edu, merlot.org, csis.org, mdpi. Pursue university cybersecurity-program resource pages, NIST/MITRE-adjacent references, and medical-device security course/reading lists (.edu resource links are durable and highly trusted).

### Avoid / do NOT do
- ❌ More generic UK-regional / off-topic guest-post syndication — low relevance for a US MedTech-security buyer.
- ❌ Any "buy backlinks / PBN / Telegram SEO" vendor — already a liability in the profile.
- ❌ New exact-match "medical device cybersecurity" anchors at scale.
- ❌ Foreign-language / non-industry guest-post farms.

**KPIs:** DR hold/grow (53 → 55+), **relevant** referring domains up (MedTech/health-IT/security), spam RD down ~370, exact-match anchor share down, and movement on commercial/informational terms — *medical device testing* (24→top 10), *medical device penetration testing* (10→top 5), *radiology information system* (25→page 1).

## 8. Strategic summary

1. **Disavow first** — ~370+ referring domains of SEOExpress/Telegram/PBN spam is the biggest liability and a reputational risk for a compliance brand.
2. **Relevance over volume** — the DR is already there; convert it by earning MedTech / healthcare-IT / security-industry links, not more UK-regional PR.
3. **Fix the anchor mix** — dilute "medical device cybersecurity" (131 RD) with branded/partial/natural.
4. **Lean into thought leadership** — bylines, HARO, a research asset, and podcast guesting are the natural fit for a B2B expert brand and earn the diverse, relevant links that move page-2 rankings to page 1.
5. **Point links at the money assets** — the compliance/blog pages that already rank 10–25 for real-volume terms.

## Bottom line

The automotive-relevant inventory on this sheet is essentially exhausted:
gmauthority and azbigmedia are already built, myautoloan is a funnel not a
publisher, and luxurylaunches is the only remaining site with a genuine
transport desk. Beyond that the fit comes from US regional news
(rutlandherald, herald-dispatch) that happens to carry car coverage.

Realistic order from this sheet: **rutlandherald $128 + herald-dispatch
$127 + luxurylaunches $325 + netlingo $109 = $689** for four, of which two
are real dailies and one has a real car desk.

For volume beyond that, go off-sheet: mine the backlink profiles of
focus2move, gmauthority and theautochannel (already built for this client)
for US automotive publishers that accept guest placements. The sheet
cannot supply more genuine auto inventory.

## Caveat that applies to rutlandherald.com — and to two earlier calls

rutlandherald.com runs a /sponsored/ directory. Example found while
checking its automotive surface:

    /sponsored/car-accident-victim-compensation-what-vermont-drivers-
      should-know/article_a7209e1c-...html

A sponsored directory is an explicit paid-link signal, the same issue
flagged on muddyrivernews.com (/business/sponsored-content/ and
/product-placement/) and medindia.net (/health-press-release/).

This qualifies two earlier recommendations in this repo: rutlandherald.com
was put forward for nangwizard.net (section 8 of the nangwizard file) and
for bluegoatcyber.com without this check having been run. The site is
still a genuine Vermont daily with real traffic and a real food desk, and
the metrics stand - but confirm with Reachgorilla that the placement lands
in editorial, not /sponsored/, before ordering it for any of the three.

## Ask Reachgorilla

Editorial placement or a sponsored silo? Of the placements inspected
across four clients, muddyrivernews.com has used /business/sponsored-
content/ and /product-placement/, medindia.net /health-press-release/,
and rutlandherald.com maintains a /sponsored/ directory. Get this
answered per domain before ordering.

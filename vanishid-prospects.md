## Excluded on judgement

**Platform and UGC domains** with inflated authority: `community.ibm.com`,
`community.openai.com` (AS 99), `community.sap.com`, `codepen.io`, `researchgate.net`,
`sourceforge.net`, `rapidapi.com`, `pressbooks.pub`, `peerlist.io`, `schoology.com`,
`indiehackers.com`. The score belongs to the platform, not your placement.

**`arabamerica.com` $300** scored well on a keyword match for "Identity" — that is ethnic and
cultural identity, not identity protection. A false positive in my own filter, excluded.

**Wrong market:** `cybersecuritynews.es` (Spain), plus the Indian, Ukrainian and Taiwanese
business press that scored well but serves no US buyer.

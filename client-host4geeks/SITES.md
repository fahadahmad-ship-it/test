## Two things to raise

### ultahost.com is a direct competitor — $350

ultahost.com sells managed VPS and WordPress hosting, the same product as
host4geeks. DR 79 against AS 42 is also the widest gap among the built
set (37). Paying a competitor $350 to host a link is a strange buy: they
control the page, they can remove or nofollow it whenever they like, and
the referral traffic it sends is to their own funnel.

### linuxsecurity.com — 4,543,529 backlinks over 6,325 referring domains

718 links per referring domain. That is sitewide or template-level
linking at a scale I have not seen elsewhere in this sheet (the next
worst is devdiscourse at 315). $300 for 2 placements there buys very
little discriminating signal.

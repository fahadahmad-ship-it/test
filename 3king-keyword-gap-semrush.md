## 7. Recommended actions

**This week**
1. **Reclaim the brand.** Establish whether `3king-game-mobile.com` is an
   affiliate, a licensee, or an unauthorised clone. If unauthorised: trademark
   complaint and a Google removal request. If affiliate: it is cannibalising your
   own branded demand and should be redirected.
2. **Run brand-defence PPC** on `3king`, `tải game 3king`, `3king tặng 30k`. At
   ~$0.6–1.5 CPC this is cheap insurance on 23,200 monthly searches while the
   organic fix lands.
3. **Build a real `3king tặng 30k` promo page.** 1,900/mo, KD 53, zero presence —
   it's your own promotion and a competitor ranks #4 for it.

**This quarter**
4. **Ship the nổ hũ long-tail cluster.** 16 keywords, KD 2–36, ~21,000/mo
   combined. Start with `nổ hũ online` (1,000, **KD 3**), `nổ hũ miễn phí`
   (1,000, KD 5), `nổ hũ 88` (2,400, **KD 3**), `nổ hũ vip` (1,300, KD 15),
   `quay hũ đổi thưởng` (590, **KD 2**). At DR 19 these are reachable with
   on-page work alone.
5. **Build the content layer.** You have 3 ranking URLs. Nothing above is
   achievable without pages to rank — this is the binding constraint, not links.
6. **Add comparison/alternative content** for the competitor-brand `đổi thưởng`
   terms (bkat, fujigame, gnbet, yo88, b52, 789 club). ~31,000/mo at KD 9–43.
7. **Re-point link budget.** Stop sending links to `/no-hu` and `/ban-ca` head
   terms. Point them at the new long-tail pages where DR 19 is actually enough.

**Deprioritise**
- `cổng game đổi thưởng` — 30/mo in Semrush.
- `bắn cá` head terms — KD 67–80, no soft entry points, your weakest cluster.
- `nổ hũ` / `tài xỉu` head terms — revisit once the long tail is landed.

# Link-Building Direction — Priority Inner Pages

**Site:** accessible.org
**Market:** United States (organic)
**Data source:** Ahrefs Site Explorer, pulled 2026-09-04
**Scope:** A focused link-building direction for 1–2 priority inner pages — target
selection, anchor direction, and source types for both AI citation and
traditional SEO value.

---

### 2b. The anchor profile is brand + spam, with almost no topical/commercial anchors

- **Branded dominates:** `Accessible.org` alone = 750 referring domains.
- **Topical anchors are thin:** `Web Accessibility Standards (WAS)` (13),
  `ADA compliance` (5), `Accessibility Statement Template` (5),
  `digital accessibility` (3).
- **Commercial anchors to service pages: effectively zero.**

### 2c. Toxic-link problem (handle in parallel — not link building, but urgent)

The backlink profile contains injected spam/PBN anchors:

- `"Running accessible.org under tight financial constraints … SEOExpress.org …"`
  — 169 ref domains, 0 dofollow (testimonial-spam injection).
- `TELEGRAM @SEO_ANOMALY - SEO BACKLINKS, PBN, TRAFFIC BOOST` — 30 ref domains.
- `High Quality Dofollow Backlinks DA 50 PA 40 Premium PBN … accessible.org` /
  `accesible.org` — ~24–30 **dofollow** links each, DR ~40 sources.

**Recommendation:** run a disavow review (domain-level) on the PBN/Telegram
clusters before or alongside the campaign. New quality links land better on a
clean profile, and the dofollow PBN links carry real risk.

## 4. Priority page selection (the 1–2 pages)

### Primary — `/services/ada-website-audits/` (money page)
- **Why:** Best commercial opportunity on the site. `ada compliance audit`
  already #8 at KD 2; the page is critically under-linked (not even in the top
  25 by referring domains). Small equity gains convert directly to a top-3
  commercial ranking.
- **Reality check:** nobody links to a sales page on its own merits. So this
  page is fed mostly by *internal* links from linkable assets, plus a small
  number of *directly earned* editorial links.

### Secondary / support — `/wcag/` (WCAG checklist)
- **Why:** The site's strongest proven link magnet (72 ref domains) and its best
  AI-citation asset (a referenceable, definitional resource). Use it as the tip
  of the spear to *earn* links, then pass equity internally down to the audit
  page.
- **Role:** link acquisition target + internal-equity conduit, not the
  conversion goal itself.

**The play:** earn links to `/wcag/` (and the other proven assets — settlement
amounts, automated-scans study), then internal-link from those high-authority
assets into `/services/ada-website-audits/` with commercial anchors. This is how
you rank a page nobody wants to link to.

## 5. Anchor direction

The profile is over-weighted to branded/URL anchors and has virtually no
commercial anchors. The correction is to introduce topical and commercial
anchors **gradually and naturally** — a spiky exact-match push on a KD-2 term
would stand out against a near-zero baseline.

Recommended distribution for **new** links to the priority pages:

| Anchor type | Target share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Branded | ~35% | `Accessible.org`, `Accessible.org's audit team` |
| Naked URL / partial | ~15% | `accessible.org/services/ada-website-audits/` |
| Generic | ~10% | `learn more`, `this guide`, `read the checklist` |
| Topical / semantic | ~25% | `ADA website audit`, `web accessibility audit`, `WCAG conformance review` |
| Exact / commercial | ~15% | `ada compliance audit`, `ADA website audit services` |

Guidance:
- Keep **exact-match `ada compliance audit`** to a minority of anchors — enough
  to signal relevance, not enough to look manufactured.
- Vary phrasing (`ADA audit`, `accessibility audit service`, `WCAG audit`) so
  the anchor cloud reads editorial.
- For `/wcag/`, lean topical: `WCAG 2.1 AA checklist`, `WCAG conformance
  checklist`, `web accessibility guidelines checklist`.

## 6. Source types — what to pursue, and why

Split by the two goals the brief calls out. There's overlap, but the emphasis
differs.

### A. For traditional SEO value (ranking authority)
- **Topically relevant editorial links** from legal/compliance, web-dev/agency,
  and B2B SaaS blogs — dofollow, in-content, contextually about ADA/WCAG.
- **Digital agency & web-developer resource pages** ("accessibility resources we
  recommend") — natural fit for the WCAG checklist.
- **Industry roundups & "best accessibility audit/company" listicles** — these
  double as citation sources (see below).
- **HARO / expert-commentary & journalist requests** on ADA-lawsuit and
  compliance stories — the lawsuit/settlement asset is perfect bait.
- **Guest contributions** on reputable compliance/legal-tech publications, with
  a contextual link to the checklist or audit page.

Prioritize *relevance and editorial context* over raw DR. A DR-40 legal-tech
blog link beats a DR-70 off-topic one for both ranking and citation.

### B. For AI citation value (LLM / AI Overviews / Perplexity / ChatGPT)
LLMs cite sources that are widely referenced, structured, and definitional.
Optimize the *asset*, not just the link:

- **Presence in high-authority industry roundups and comparison pages** — the
  sources AI systems retrieve and quote when asked "best ADA audit companies."
  (Aligns with the `/digital-accessibility-companies/` example.)
- **Wikipedia / Wikidata / authoritative reference citations** for
  ADA/WCAG-related entities — hard to earn, high citation leverage.
- **Original data & statistics** (settlement amounts, the "13% of WCAG criteria"
  automated-scans study). LLMs preferentially cite quantified, original data —
  the site already earns links to exactly these pages; amplify them.
- **Structured, definitional content** (`what is a VPAT`, `WCAG 2.1 AA
  checklist`) with clear headings, schema markup, and quotable definitions.
- **Mentions in listicles, directories, and Q&A/forum content** (Reddit,
  industry forums) — increasingly part of AI retrieval, even when nofollow.

**Key point:** AI citation rewards being *the quotable, original source*.
Traditional SEO rewards the *editorial dofollow link*. The overlap — data-driven
resource pages that earn editorial links — is where to concentrate, and the
WCAG checklist + lawsuit/settlement assets already sit in that overlap.

## 7. Suggested sequencing

1. **Disavow review** of the PBN/Telegram spam clusters (parallel track).
2. **Internal linking pass:** from `/wcag/`, `/ada-website-compliance-lawsuit-settlement-amounts/`,
   and `/automated-scans-wcag/` into `/services/ada-website-audits/` with topical/commercial anchors.
3. **Earn links to `/wcag/`** (agency resource pages, roundups, guest posts) —
   the tip of the spear.
4. **A small set of directly earned editorial links** to
   `/services/ada-website-audits/` using the anchor mix in §5.
5. **Amplify the data assets** (settlement amounts, scans study) for AI citation
   via digital-PR / journalist outreach.
6. **Fix VPAT targeting** so the 5,100-volume demand routes toward the
   `/services/vpat/` money page.

## Patterns across 48 domains

**1. High authority is inversely correlated with buyability.** The four highest-AS domains (58, 53, 52, 49) all failed — no contributor surface, a grey-hat tool page, an abused Moodle instance, an injection farm. The three buys sit at AS 53, 40 and 36.

**2. Trust Flow actively misleads here.** The highest TF values on the sheet — activeprospect 58, rswebsols 56, trueconf 46, scoop 45, cgmagonline 45 — are all rejects. rswebsols carries TF 56 and **0.5% US traffic**. Both Tier 1/Tier 3 buys sit at TF 33.

**3. The decisive test is: does commercial filler out-link the genuine editorial?** It killed 11 domains that passed every numeric gate, including sites where real content exists but sits below the bought pages.

**4. One syndicated advertorial catalogue appears across unrelated owners.** Casino and sports-betting odds, attorney lead-gen, buy-Instagram-followers and "best AI courses 2026" turn up in a Vermont daily (Sample News Group), a North Carolina daily (Paxton), and two unaffiliated student papers. The vendor is reselling slots in a shared advertorial network, not editorial placements.

**5. `.ac.uk` in a commercial guest-post sheet is itself the flag.** Three universities tested across this session: Cranfield's only surface was a student/library blog; Essex's Moodle carries a tag page reading *"buy edu gov backlinks"*; Henley is genuinely clean and has nothing to sell.

**6. The cybersecurity category is exhausted.** The client has bought essentially all of it — one unbought infosec domain remained when this round began.

## Anchor allocation

Thirteen anchors were supplied; only three placements are buyable, so most anchors have no home on this sheet.

- **Tier 1** (exact-match technical) — only `devopsschool.com` qualifies, carrying `SaMD cybersecurity`. SaMD is a secure-SDLC topic, so it reads natively to an OWASP/DevSecOps audience. **`IEC 81001-5-1`, `FDA cybersecurity documentation` and `medical device cybersecurity assessment` have no suitable home here** — placing them on general tech or local news would be an auditable paid-link tell.

- **Tier 3** — `skillsyouneed.com` carries `Blue Goat Cyber`.

The existing `fingerlakes1.com` link shows the failure mode to avoid: an exact-match **"medical device software testing company"** anchor on a generic *"3 Essential Characteristics of a Successful Business"* post. That mismatch is itself the tell.

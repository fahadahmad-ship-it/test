## The strategic finding

**This vendor's inventory cannot serve this client's main need, and the competitors prove why.**

A sweep of 10 direct competitors' referring domains (vulcan7, theredx, landvoice, mojosells, theclose, realgeeks, followupboss, boldtrail, chime.me, ylopo) intersected against all 11,020 inventory rows returned 89 raw matches. After stripping platform/profile links (crunchbase, behance, clickup, sourceforge, vocal.media), link-insertion mills (msn, usatoday, businessinsider, cnet) and sites failing a content sanity-check, **6-8 are actionable and only 2-3 would be read by a working US agent.**

The structural reason matters more than the list: **these competitors did not build their links by buying guest posts.** Their profiles are SaaS directories (g2, getapp, saashub), MLS and brokerage sites, podcast-syndication networks (vulcan7, mojosells and theredx all run shows), and data-scraper domains. There is no repeatable purchasable guest-post route hiding in them.

**17 intersection domains were excluded as already-owned — every single one is home/decor/architecture** (urbansplatter, thearchitectsdiary, re-thinkingthefuture, gharpedia, civiconcepts, e-architect, amazingarchitecture, pinuphouses, lorddecor, tinyhouse, thepinnaclelist). The competitors are picking up the same decor inventory the client already bought. That lane is saturated.

Most purchasable competitor profile: `theclose.com`, then `ylopo.com` and `followupboss.com`. Least: `boldtrail.com` and `chime.me` — near-pure brokerage/MLS/directory, zero buyable overlap.

## Method note — the live index under-reports

Of four order-list domains checked live, three (homebusinessmag, e-architect, amazingarchitecture) appeared in both tools, but **fancyhouse-design.com — the newest order, Sept '26 — returned ERROR 50 NOTHING FOUND in Semrush and was absent from Ahrefs**, on a control-validated filter. The index misses the newest links specifically, so the client's order list is authoritative and live duplication is a secondary check only.

Semrush `display_filter` must be a real JSON array with the `sign` key: `[{"field":"refdomain","operation":"contains","sign":"+","value":"<domain>"}]`. A JSON-encoded string errors; omitting `sign` silently returns an unfiltered dump that reads as "no match". Control for this client: `homebusinessmag` (→ `/expired-listings`, anchor "expired listing leads", dofollow).

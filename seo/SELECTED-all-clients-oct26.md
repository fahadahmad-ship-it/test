## Cross-client notes

**johnsoncountypost.com and sanangelolive.com are selected for two clients each.** Both are real local papers that run multiple verticals, so this is workable — but space the placements and use different sections and authors.

**Reject-by-chain, confirmed this round.** Two newspaper groups resell the same silo vendor:
- **Boone Newsmedia** — `/sponsored-content/<slug>-<hash>`: austindailyherald, albertleatribune, lagrangenews, natchezdemocrat, suffolknewsherald. Inventory: EssayPro, buy-YouTube-views, buy-Google-reviews, 1win casino, Melbet, kratom, payday.
- **Sample News Group** — TownNews BLOX, `/sponsored/`: rutlandherald, newsitem, thedailyreview, oswegocountynewsnow.

**Two fast disqualifying screens**, both of which caught sites that passed every other gate:
1. **Dofollow share of linked domains under 75%** — killed designrush (0.61%), fonts2u (0.34%), stockhouse (1.2%), opencart (38.8%), ilounge (43.7%), hostadvice (55.9%).
2. **Root-level injection search** — `casino`, `essay`, `betting`, `kratom`, `apk`, `payday`, `buy-youtube`, `downloader` anywhere in the URL, not just under `/sponsored/`. Caught ruby-doc.org (casino blog on the Ruby docs domain), cyberpanel, spacedaily, montereyherald, thedailynewsonline, psu.com, netlingo.

**Check live traffic before authority.** realtybiznews at DR 63 draws 8 visits/month; tuaw at DR 76 has 81% of its referring domains dead. A high Majestic TF against a low Semrush AS usually means Majestic is scoring a corpse.

**What a big masthead actually sells.** usatoday.com's `/press-release/story/<id>/` annex and theglobeandmail.com's `/partners/` both return **zero ranking keywords**. A link from a zero-equity annex on a DR 92 domain is worth about what one from a DR 20 domain is worth.

## 5. Pre-payment checks

1. **The $51–52 Newsquest prices are far below market** for AS 50 / 230k UK traffic titles (normally $300–800). Confirm before paying: **dofollow**, page **indexable** (not a noindex `/sponsored/` path), editorial placement rather than a syndicated partner-content template.
2. **therugbypaper** — confirm dofollow explicitly (56% of its profile is nofollow) and ask whether placement is editorial or `/guest-blogs/`.
3. **Dorset Echo and Evening News 24 are both Newsquest** — they're split across Sets 1 and 2 deliberately. Don't add a third Newsquest title.
4. **Pitch angle by site type.** Newsquest titles frame vaping around health risk, so lead with **smoking cessation / tobacco harm reduction**, not product promotion. Football and rugby titles take a lifestyle/"switching" angle. `mayfair-london` already ran "luxury vaping trends" — mirror that register. `islandecho` is near-virgin on outbound links, so a generic SEO guest post will stand out; pitch local-interest or cessation.
5. **Anchor spread across all 12:** roughly 3 exact ("nicotine pouches"), 5 partial/branded, 4 URL/brand. Keep exact-match concentrated in the lower-AS sports titles, not the Newsquest anchors.

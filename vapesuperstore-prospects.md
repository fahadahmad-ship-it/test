## The thing that should drive this campaign

**The Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026 received Royal Assent on 29 April 2026.** Advertising and
sponsorship of vaping and nicotine products ends across the UK from **1 June 2027** — about
eight months away.

Separately, the current CAP Code *already* prohibits marketing nicotine-containing e-cigarettes
that are not licensed as medicines across most non-broadcast media. CAP has issued an
Enforcement Notice and is running focused monitoring on vape advertising.

A paid guest post promoting vape products on a UK publisher is a marketing communication in
non-broadcast media. That is the reason mainstream UK publishers refuse this inventory, and it
is visible in your own sheet — the built profile leans on small regional titles, overseas
publishers and vape trade press rather than UK nationals.

Practical consequences:

- **UK product-led placements carry live regulatory risk to the publisher**, which is the main
  cause of rejection. Pitch brand, industry, harm-reduction or retail-business angles rather
  than product marketing, and link to informational pages rather than product collections.
- **The window closes June 2027.** Anything dependent on UK vape advertising should be
  front-loaded well before then.
- I am reading the public guidance, not advising on it. Have whoever handles your compliance
  confirm the position before committing to a UK campaign.

## Two things I would not buy

**University domains.** The shortlist surfaced `ucl.ac.uk` $260, `bath.ac.uk` $260,
`essex.ac.uk` $250, `wlv.ac.uk` $250, `blogs.brighton.ac.uk` $600 — and your sheet already
carries `scu.edu` and `life.edu`. A paid .ac.uk or .edu link to a vape retailer is both an
obvious paid-link footprint and a nicotine brand attaching itself to a student audience.
Given that vape marketing must not appeal to under-18s, this is the worst category on the sheet
for this client. I have excluded all seven.

**Young-skewing audiences.** `esports-news.co.uk` $400, `geektown.co.uk` $300,
`cultbox.co.uk` $370 and football fan sites like `bluemoon-mcfc.co.uk` $550 all score well on
metrics, but the under-18 appeal test makes them poor choices for a nicotine brand. Excluded
for the same reason.

## Caveats

Metrics are the marketplace sheet's own figures, not re-verified per domain. I contacted no
publisher, and the sheet has no restricted-niche column — in a regulated category that is the
single biggest unknown, so confirm vape acceptance with the broker before planning spend.

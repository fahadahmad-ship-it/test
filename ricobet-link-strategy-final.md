# ricobet.com.mx — Effective 5-Backlink Strategy (FINAL)
Supersedes all earlier ricobet link plans. Built on the audit of the 404
placements already delivered. Semrush MX metrics.

---

## 1. WHAT CHANGED, AND WHY

Earlier plans optimised **keyword → anchor → target**. The delivery CSV proves
that was never the failing variable:

- `Ricobet` → `/` — **already bought. Not indexed.**
- `casino online mexico` → `/` — **already bought ×6. Not indexed.**
- `bono sin depósito casino méxico` → `/promotion` — **already bought ×2. Not indexed.**

404 placements, 99% unindexed, 18% on dead pages, inventory sitting on an
interlinked casino PBN (`mrcasinomy`, `admiral-xcasino`, `rouletteyy`,
`maxgameon`). The targeting was fine. **The pages the links sat on were worthless.**

**So the strategy changes axis: from WHICH KEYWORD to WHICH PAGE THE LINK SITS ON.**

### One useful consequence
Because 99% of those links were never indexed, **Google has never seen them**.
There is no anchor-text footprint to work around and no penalty to unwind. The
profile Google actually counts is very small. Re-using an anchor that "failed" is
not a risk — it never registered.

## 2. THE STRATEGY IN ONE LINE

**Five indexed, editorial, Mexican placements — concentrated on the homepage to
lift domain Authority Score, plus the one page that has no links at all.**

Not five keywords. Not five rankings. At AS 17 against competitors at AS 26–40,
five links cannot rank a KD 60–81 term, and any plan claiming otherwise is wrong.
What five *good* links can do is move domain authority and prove the channel works.

**3 × homepage.** Domain AS 17 is the demonstrated gate — `versusbet.mx` ranks in
the target SERP at **AS 26 on fewer referring domains (1,119 vs 1,224) and a
quarter the follow links (378 vs 1,515)**. Ricobet has more links and less
authority, so quality is the only variable left. The homepage is also the only URL
on the site that converts equity into rankings (6 keywords, 1,392 visits; every
other page ranks for nothing).

**1 × `/livecasino`.** The only target with **zero backlinks**. Every other page is
buried under hundreds of inert links, so this is the one place a single link has
uncontested marginal value. Highest CPC in the account at **$14.37**.

**1 × `/promotion`.** Deliberately kept as a controlled experiment: does **one
indexed, quality link** do what **221 unindexed ones** did not? That is worth
knowing before any larger order. Best keyword in the account (6,600/KD60) on an
operator-friendly SERP.

## 4. THE PLACEMENT SPEC — THIS IS THE ACTUAL STRATEGY

The previous 404 links failed here, not on targeting. Every criterion below is
pass/fail, verified client-side, with no vendor discretion.

| # | Criterion | Requirement | Why (from the audit) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Indexed** | Linking URL present in Google's index **before** payment, re-checked day 14 | **400/404 were "Not indexed"** |
| 2 | **Page has real traffic** | ≥100 organic visits/mo to the **linking page** (Semrush) | PBN pages have none |
| 3 | **Page ranks for something** | Linking page ranks top-50 for ≥1 keyword with ≥100 vol | Hard proof Google trusts it |
| 4 | **Not a casino PBN** | Host must not be a gambling blog whose outbound links go mainly to other casino blogs | Inventory was `mrcasinomy` / `rouletteyy` / `maxgameon` class |
| 5 | **Live, not dead** | HTTP 200 at day 0, 30, 60, 90 | **72 of 404 were 404/dead** |
| 6 | Language / audience | Spanish, ≥60% Mexico traffic | — |
| 7 | Domain authority | Semrush AS ≥30 | Ricobet is AS 17; links must be *above* it |
| 8 | Link position | In-body prose, first 60% of article | — |
| 9 | Link type | Dofollow, `rel` free of nofollow/sponsored/ugc | — |
| 10 | Outbound links | ≤25 external; ≤3 to other gambling domains | PBN tell |

**Criteria 1–4 are new and are the whole point.** Criteria 6–10 were in the
previous brief and did not prevent this failure on their own.

**This is a calibration run, not a ranking campaign.** State that up front.

**At 12 weeks, check three things:**
1. Are all 5 placements still indexed and live? *(Previous benchmark: 1%.)*
2. Has domain Authority Score moved off **17**?
3. Has `/livecasino` acquired any ranking at all — it starts from zero links and
   zero keywords, so it is the cleanest read available.

**If all 5 stay indexed and AS moves even 1–2 points**, the channel works and the
real programme is 8–12 placements/month to close an AS 17 → 26+ gap.

**If AS does not move with 5 genuinely indexed links**, the constraint is not
off-site at all, and the next investigation is indexation and content on
ricobet's own pages — not more links.
